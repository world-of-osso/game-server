# Toy Box server foundation

## Contract

- Toy membership comes only from local Retail 12.1.0.69933 Toy.db2. Import every decrypted row, retain unresolved item/spell joins, and expose them as unavailable. No synthetic membership or spells.
- UseItem on a Retail toy learns it for the authenticated account, consuming one physical item in the same redb transaction. Duplicate learning is rejected without consumption. Unsupported activation does not prevent learning.
- Learned state and favourite flags belong to the account, survive database reopen, and reach every online character of that account. Other accounts do not inherit them.
- SetToyFavourite requires learned ownership. World entry and every change deliver a full ToyCollectionUpdate. Catalog entries include item/spell identity, name/icon, source, flags, expansion, learned/favourite state and an explicit unavailable reason.
- UseToy requires learned ownership and item class/race/level eligibility. It dispatches through the existing authoritative spell pipeline, including caster state, target validation, cast time, spell and category history. ItemEffect nonnegative recovery/category durations override SpellCooldowns; negative values retain spell durations. SpellCooldownUpdate reports cooldowns; ToyResult answers start/rejection. SpellGo/CastFailed retain later cast outcome.
- Unresolved spells, unsupported effect/aura types, script-only toy behavior and unsupported ItemEffect conditions/specialization restrictions fail explicitly before starting a cast or cooldown. Zero-entry transform special cases are unsupported, not successful cosmetic no-ops.
- Audited visual-only spells require a nonzero DifficultyID-0 SpellXSpellVisual binding and exclusively zero-valued dummy/script effects or dummy auras, with no trigger, misc payload, item creation or mechanic. They deliver SpellGo with caster/hit targets and apply/replicate/cancel their auras normally. A visual binding alone never authorizes a gameplay script or periodic dummy.
- Audited attachment toys include Spitzy, Worn Cloak, Filmless Camera, Lampyridae Lure, Valdekar's Special and Lil' Scoots' Pillow. Ken-Ken's Mask applies to the selected friendly target, not automatically the caster. Mallard Mortar delivers its targeted visual/squeak after the authentic 2000ms cast without causing damage. Preserve each DB2 aura lifetime and normal cancellation; no creature spawn or global transform is inferred from an attachment.
- Duck-Stuffed Duck Lovie194056/spell383065 is visual-only: authentic visual118994 supplies the held nap animation, duck attachment and cast sound. Its zero-valued dummy aura follows ordinary application/replication/cancellation; do not invent healing, transformation or gameplay sleep effects.
- Akazamzarak's Spare Hat147838/239560 uses its authored2000ms cast and5000ms hat/rabbit aura; the rabbit is visual art, not a summoned creature. Barrier Generator153183/254513 retains its10000ms channel and visual aura, without damage absorption or collision. A Drake's Big Basket of Eggs204675/405634 retains its conditional mount visual bindings and ordinary aura cancellation, without globally changing the player model or replacing the mount.
- Iron Belly Spirits88587/128327 uses its1000ms cast and missile to apply its cosmetic refreshment aura to the selected friendly target for60000ms; no healing or resource gain. Darkshard Fragment131812/196783 applies crystal-growth attachments to the caster for5000ms, not a model transform. Aqir Egg Cluster175063/318453 uses its authored missile trigger318452 to apply15000ms swarm attachments to the selected target, not guardian creatures or damage. Retain normal source targeting, cooldowns, aura cancellation and expiry; only the three complete zero-valued cosmetic aura spells are audited.
- Aura260 ScreenEffect with a nonzero screen ID is client-owned state: apply/replicate its spell identity and normal lifetime/cancellation. Do not create a gameplay script or infer support for the spell's other effects.
- Silversage Incense88381/127771 and Totem of Harmony88584/128180 summon their authentic TDB1210 scenery65298/65387, using source displays and destination87, remaining stationary and expiring after20000/60000ms. Prepare missing templates/models with `scripts/add_toy_summon_templates.py`; do not invent interaction, healing, guardian attacks or substitute displays. Other missing/scripted summons remain unavailable.
- Source-complete stationary war/seasonal banners and Artisan's Sign197719 require authentic Retail item definitions and TDB1210 scenery, never substitute items or actors. Banner destination47 uses its authored radius (war banners1yd, seasonal banners2yd) ahead of the caster; sign destination138 uses the current destination. Preserve wild template faction, faction-restricted item eligibility, normal60000ms banner lifetime and the sign's2000ms cast/300000ms lifetime. Prepare with the same scenery importer; custom sign text, interaction, combat, vehicle and unproved scaled scenery remain unavailable. [Selected identities](../wiki/systems/toys.md#toy-effects9-stationary-banners-and-sign-2026-10-10).
- Temporary wild summons preserve template faction unless SummonProperties overrides it; ally/guardian/companion summons inherit the caster faction. Companions follow their caster, are immune to player/NPC attacks and replace an occupied positive summon slot. Missing creature/property identities and puppet/vehicle/specialized categories remain explicit unavailable reasons.

- Aura61 MOD_SCALE adds all active per-stack percentage points to native object scale, with Retail minima0.1 for players and0.01 for creatures. Replicated UnitScale updates existing/new visuals and restores native scale on removal. Reference: TrinityCore a352b1fa Unit::RecalculateObjectScale, SpellAuraEffects::HandleAuraModScale.

- Aura105 FEATHER_FALL replicates authoritative FeatherFall state, caps descending server and client movement at7yd/s (TrinityCore MovementUtil.cpp terminalSafefallVelocity), negates landing damage, and restores normal gravity on removal. Client-reported heights are capped per server tick, not per input. Removing the last aura restarts fall accounting at current height (TC a352b1fa HandleAuraFeatherFall).

- Aura37 EFFECT_IMMUNITY prevents only the spell effect ID in MiscValue, including friendly summon offers, at target filtering and effect application. Spell/effect NoImmunity attributes bypass it. Ordinary UnitAuras transport/presentation and removal restore eligibility; unsupported puppet/script effects remain blocked. References: TC a352b1fa SpellInfo::LoadImmunityInfo, Unit::IsImmunedToSpellEffect.

- Aura293 OVERRIDE_SPELLS loads MiscValue's ordered10-spell OverrideSpellData record from local CASC. AuraView.overrides carries the list using existing aura332 transport; the Retail six-button main-bar override replaces even empty/item slots without rebinding. Only active set spells gain temporary server cast permission; removal restores base slots/permissions. Missing override records/spells stay unavailable; underlying ability scripts are not implemented by swapping buttons. References: TC a352b1fa HandleAuraOverrideSpells; cached Retail OverrideActionBar.lua:42/Setup; WoWDBDefs layout297CBDC8.

- Aura226 with a nonzero DB2 period and trigger runs a data-backed triggered cast on each due tick, retaining a channel's original target. Script hooks retain ownership of scripted effects. Missing trigger/period and conditional consuming triggers remain unsupported; cosmetic appearance alone does not prove a periodic no-op.
- Aura321 prevents casts whose SpellCategories.PreventionType includes NO_ACTIONS (4), interrupts those active casts on application, and restores eligibility after the last such aura leaves. MiscValue is not the prevention mask (authentic toy values include0 and85). Reference: TC a352b1fa HandleAuraModNoActions, SharedDefines.h.

- Aura428 LINKED_SUMMON makes the aura target cast its DB2 summon trigger on first application. Refresh does not duplicate it; removal despawns one matching owned creature per summon effect, without removing another owner's summon. Missing trigger/creature/properties and unsupported summon controls remain blocked. Authentic linked toy templates/models/addons must be prepared from local TDB data, not synthesized. Reference: TC a352b1fa HandleLinkedSummon.
- Tent summons support the fully data-defined destination pair18/79: caster destination followed by a backward offset using the nonzero DB2 radius. Other destination-back forms remain unavailable. Reference: TC a352b1fa SelectImplicitDestDestTargets.
- Aura258 remains unavailable when transmitted-gameobject/fishing behavior or replacement object templates are missing. Its MiscValue0/1 replaces a summoned gameobject entry; it is not a linked creature summon (TC a352b1fa EffectTransmitted).

- Effect50 TRANS_DOOR uses actual local Retail tdb_gameobject_template and DB2 display bounds. Non-scripted generic decorations, chairs and mailboxes spawn once at the selected destination with template scale/facing/map and spell lifetime (whole seconds); normal cast completion does not remove them. Chairs use the existing seat interaction. Rituals retain their channel-owned handler. Missing templates/displays, custom scripts and other object interactions remain unavailable before cooldowns.
- Target87 DEST_DEST preserves a requested or earlier selected destination, or uses the caster's position when none was selected (TC SelectImplicitDestDestTargets/CheckDst). It is not ground-height snapping. Native UseToy has no ground-picking destination field; its target87 objects spawn at the caster.
- Aura233 CHANGE_MODEL_FOR_ALL_HUMANOIDS is viewer-local, client-owned appearance. Apply/replicate its spell identity and normal duration/cancellation; never change global ModelDisplay or infer support for other effects. Rendered viewer-local models require client consumption, outside this server change.

## Exclusions

Toy Box UI, search/filter/pagination, action-bar persistence, scripted toy actions and rendered client visual proof are separate work. Existing mount/pet character storage is unchanged. Toy cooldowns follow existing character SpellHistory lifetime; persistence of cooldowns across server restart is not provided by this foundation.
