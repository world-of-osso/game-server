# Havoc DPS parity (577)

This page records Havoc-specific differences from the pinned SimC `76f8118` reference. The shared behavior contract is [DPS parity](dps-parity.md).

## What it must do

- [x] Burning Blades uses 35% of eligible landed damage, including critical damage. Exergy must not apply again to the residual when it already affected the source hit.
- [x] Vengeful Retreat keeps its native 70448 movement and the actor returns at its native run speed. Havoc parity and follower drivers walk to maximum melee reach and face away before casting, so the leap travels through the target.
- [x] Immolation Aura tick 258922 uses periodic proc eligibility, so it does not consume Guillotine's 1291728 RPPM attempts.
- [x] Perfected Guillotine 1306624 hits its selected targets together after one second, matching SimC rather than serializing the native bouncy-chain attribute.
- [x] Eye Beam and Abyssal Gaze select their normal or empowered tick at channel start. Buff expiry or gain during the channel does not change that selection, including Final Breath.

## Retreat comparison

Seed 70448, 1T, 300 seconds; the native result includes the stat and Hunt fixes in train `777281ccf7`. SimC uses its pinned hardcoded movement model (`sc_demon_hunter.cpp:86,1552–1618,8592–8630`); native values come from spell 198793 and actual movement.

| Term | Native | SimC |
|---|---:|---:|
| Leap displacement | 17.88 yd observed | 20 yd |
| First return distance outside melee | 15.05 yd | 3.8 yd |
| Return speed | 9.7–10.2 yd/s observed | about 9.15 yd/s |
| Melee reach | 5.33 yd, native combat reaches | 9 yd, 5 yd plus target reach 4 yd |
| Total out of melee | 29.0 s | 7.275 s |

SimC uses a 1.8 movement-direction factor and automatically returns; none of the twelve out-of-range intervals uses Felblade or Fel Rush. Native Felfire Haste contributes its 10% speed; SimC marks that talent unimplemented. With the max-reach setup, the real 300-second native seed-70448 test records 12 Retreats and 13.300 seconds outside melee, including the out-of-range part of the physical leap. The accepted remainder reflects SimC's simplified movement model. Do not change native spell physics to hide it.

At train landing `87c4916e4d`, raw Havoc was 200,929 vs 214,737 DPS (1T, −6.43%) and 312,460 vs 334,847 DPS (3T, −6.69%). The earlier 1T estimate of 10,200 DPS from extra melee downtime gives approximately 211,129 DPS (−1.68%) if excluded; this is an extrapolation, **not a measured counterfactual**. No corresponding 3T estimate is established.

A counterfactual copy of `76f8118` removes only Exergy parsing for Burning Blades spell 453177, the extra Eternal Hunt factor on both final Blade Dance/Death Sweep strike implementations, and the bugs-mode Soulscar Demon Hide factor. The MID2 Havoc profile uses the same 300-second encounter settings with diagnostic seeds 70448, 1, and 2, with one SimC iteration per seed. These are three-sample means, not the 1,000-iteration reference above. The landed native train used ten seeds (70448 and 1–9), not three.

| Targets | Pinned SimC, same seeds | SimC without double applications | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 214,153.498 | 207,675.513 | −6,477.985 |
| 3 | 351,898.916 | 344,094.667 | −7,804.249 |

For 3T, the counterfactual changes Burning Blades by −3,409.74 DPS, Death Sweep's final primary and secondary hits by −2,930.63 and −836.60, and Blade Dance's final primary and secondary hits by −467.53 and −159.75. Other actions are unchanged, including Soulscar for this profile. The apparent increase from the 334,847 DPS reference is sampling: that reference averages 1,000 iterations, not these three seeds. The narrow sample also limits confidence in the resulting parity comparison.

The counterfactual run with the original 1,000-iteration reference settings gives **207,943.699 DPS (1T)** and **327,182.617 DPS (3T)**. At train landing `7feeaa7887`, native Havoc is **194,849.415** and **305,352.662 DPS**, or **−6.30%** and **−6.67%** against that control. Against the original pinned 1,000-iteration SimC reference, the raw differences are −9.26% and −8.81%. Neither comparison excludes Retreat geometry.

At the later Immolation proc and Guillotine timing landing `7caa306da4`, native measured 194,706.589 DPS (1T) and 308,580.074 DPS (3T), or −6.37% and −5.69% against the 1,000-iteration counterfactual. This predates the max-reach Retreat technique.

At Retreat technique landing `200a78331b`, the ten-seed native means are **201,653.753 DPS (1T)** and **310,379.262 DPS (3T)**. Against the 1,000-iteration no-double control, the raw differences are **−3.02%** and **−5.14%**. Against original pinned SimC they are −6.09% and −7.31%. The largest 3T deficit against the no-double control is Demonsurge 452416: 53,007 native versus 57,887 SimC DPS, a 4,880 DPS gap.

Against the three-seed counterfactual at `7feeaa7887`, native differences are −6.18% and −11.26%; this 3T sample is too noisy to use as the parity reference. Its largest per-spell deficits are Demonsurge (452416), 3,080 DPS in 1T, and Perfected Guillotine (1306624), 10,091 DPS in 3T; these rankings do not establish a cause.

## SimC double applications

- SimC deposits 35% of an already Exergy-modified landed hit into Burning Blades, then applies Exergy to the residual tick again (`sc_demon_hunter.cpp:3088–3110`; residual action tick multiplier). Do not copy the second application.
- SimC applies Eternal Hunt twice to final Blade Dance/Death Sweep strikes (`sc_demon_hunter.cpp:7055–7065,7113–7121`). Apply the native modifier once.
- SimC bugs-mode Soulscar double-applies Demon Hide (`sc_demon_hunter.cpp:8486–8501`). Do not copy it or apply that Soulscar-specific behavior to The Hunt.
