# Havoc DPS parity (577)

This page records Havoc-specific differences from the pinned SimC `76f8118` reference. The shared behavior contract is [DPS parity](dps-parity.md).

## What it must do

- [x] Burning Blades uses 35% of eligible landed damage, including critical damage. Exergy must not apply again to the residual when it already affected the source hit.
- [x] Vengeful Retreat keeps its native 70448 movement and the actor returns at its native run speed. SimC's different return geometry is not a native spell-data error.

## Retreat comparison

Seed 70448, 1T, 300 seconds; the native result includes the stat and Hunt fixes in train `777281ccf7`. SimC uses its pinned hardcoded movement model (`sc_demon_hunter.cpp:86,1552–1618,8592–8630`); native values come from spell 198793 and actual movement.

| Term | Native | SimC |
|---|---:|---:|
| Leap displacement | 17.88 yd observed | 20 yd |
| First return distance outside melee | 15.05 yd | 3.8 yd |
| Return speed | 9.7–10.2 yd/s observed | about 9.15 yd/s |
| Melee reach | 5.33 yd, native combat reaches | 9 yd, 5 yd plus target reach 4 yd |
| Total out of melee | 29.0 s | 7.275 s |

SimC uses a 1.8 movement-direction factor and automatically returns; none of the twelve out-of-range intervals uses Felblade or Fel Rush. Native Felfire Haste contributes its 10% speed; SimC marks that talent unimplemented. The remaining difference is SimC's geometric simplification. Do not change native spell physics to hide it.

At train landing `87c4916e4d`, raw Havoc was 200,929 vs 214,737 DPS (1T, −6.43%) and 312,460 vs 334,847 DPS (3T, −6.69%). The earlier 1T estimate of 10,200 DPS from extra melee downtime gives approximately 211,129 DPS (−1.68%) if excluded; this is an extrapolation, **not a measured counterfactual**. No corresponding 3T estimate is established.

## SimC double applications

- SimC deposits 35% of an already Exergy-modified landed hit into Burning Blades, then applies Exergy to the residual tick again (`sc_demon_hunter.cpp:3088–3110`; residual action tick multiplier). Do not copy the second application.
- SimC bugs-mode Soulscar double-applies Demon Hide (`sc_demon_hunter.cpp:8486–8501`). Do not copy it or apply that Soulscar-specific behavior to The Hunt.
