# Blood Death Knight parity

Icy Talons increases attack speed but does not increase per-swing damage. Retail `WoWCombatLog-102526_094013.txt` records the same Blood player and target at equal attack power: noncritical swings of 13,476 at three stacks and 13,446 at zero stacks, 1.4 seconds apart. Pinned SimC `76f8118` models the speed effect.
