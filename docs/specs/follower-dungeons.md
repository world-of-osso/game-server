## Full-spec follower kits (user decision, 2026-10-18)

`FOLLOWER_OWNER_TARGET_AURA_STATE_EXEMPTION_VIA_SUMMONED_BY`: followers use the project's existing `UnitSummonedBy(owner)` representation. Consequently the shared backend's source owner-target aura-state exemption applies when healing that owner; this follower modelling choice is project policy, not Retail follower-dungeon parity. The source relation is TC a352b1fa `SpellInfo.cpp:2497` `!unitCaster->IsVehicle() && !(unitCaster->GetCharmerOrOwner() == target)`; `Unit.h:1208,1233–1238` derives owner from SummonedBy and prefers charmer. Charmer ownership is currently unmodelled.

Interrupt provider runs once per kit update, at the first available native interrupt-capable entry, retaining higher-priority actions. Explicit kicks have priority5; below Rebuke's native availability, Avenger's Shield retains its attack-priority position after emergency healing. Demonology retains its existing pre-action learned-pet kick. The kit hands off only its already-selected enemy, preserving the former embedded-slot target scope; the provider's ordered multi-candidate API is reserved for explicit callers. No planner/navigation/retarget change. Eligibility uses the shared live `spell_cast::can_interrupt_cast` with the actual casting unit, never the pushback flag. Ordinary native spell execution retains costs, motion and hit authority.

Delivery is sliced: core rotations/heals/defensives/emergencies first; sourced dispel/interrupt/CC conditions follow separately. Full-kit completion remains open, including immunities, movement and pet selection. Inventory or existing narrow interrupt behavior does not close those gaps.

Moonfire/Sunfire maintenance must test the native periodic child aura, not the dummy root. With no native trigger edge (`ROOT_CHILD_LINK_DESCRIPTION_ONLY`), the COPY producer parses70448 description `$<spellid>` references and requires exactly one existing same-name periodic-damage aura. Missing or ambiguous matches fail explicitly. Record per-kit-row description/name/effect provenance and verify the consumed fields against pinned CSVs; change only the three maintenance rows, never priorities, timers or coefficients.

All eleven followers receive their native default player-spec starter spellbook at their own level. `follower_spec_kit(spec_id,spell_id,min_level,max_level,role_slot,priority,conditions,policy)` is additive: an absent table or a specialization with no rows retains the four-slot/class-defensive bridge. A populated specialization never substitutes those legacy spells when its kit is level-gated or temporarily unready. Casts retain ordinary costs, GCD, cooldowns, charges, aura prerequisites, range, LOS and effect execution; no new damage/heal amount or timer is permitted.

Native build12.1.5.70448 inputs: SpecializationSpells, SpellLevels, SkillLineAbility and the default TraitTreeLoadout/Entry with TraitDefinition. Talent grants, source currency budgets and TraitCond/edge/level eligibility use the existing player `starter_purchases`/`build_config` validator in authored entry order. Only affordable eligible purchases and native granted entries contribute. SpellLevels additionally gates each selected spell. Receipt includes every selected level1–90 loadout, purchases/grants, unspent currencies, parsed native trees and input hashes. Talent passives use the existing passive-aura path, not bespoke follower stat grants. Kit actors carry their sourced `ActiveSpec`, so native spec-gated scripts such as Demonology Shadow Bolt generate their ordinary resource; no `Player` component or resource grant is synthesized.

`FOLLOWER_BATTLEFIELD_PRIORITY_V1` is a **named project policy, not a Retail rotation**. Lower priority numbers win; spell ID breaks ties. Conditions and thresholds are deliberately project-authored:

- **Emergency and sustain policy:** interrupts5; Lay on Hands/Guardian Spirit10 below20% health; strong direct heals20 below40%; Ironbark25 below50% on an attacked party member; personal defensives30 below50% while attacked. Missing auras prevent redundant protection. Healer Lifebloom45 on an injured tank below95%, one active party application; Rejuvenation/Prayer of Mending50 below90%; group heals55 when at least three party members are below70%; Regrowth/Flash Heal65 below80%; non-healer off-heals70 below35%. Native aura/cooldown restrictions still decide whether a cast is legal.
- **Tank policy:** lost-threat taunts35, missing active mitigation40, group/self buffs75, combat cooldowns80, ground attacks85, other area attacks90, damage-over-time maintenance100, spenders/cooldown attacks110, fillers150. Existing interrupts and emergency heals may replace a cast only after ordinary replacement validation succeeds. No spell is granted merely because its name appears in the policy.
- **Area policy:** Consecration and Thunder Clap may serve one engaged enemy; other selected area attacks require three. Native radii and actual caster/target/owned-field centers determine coverage. A moving tank does not refresh a field that still covers its enemies; an enemy leaving the field may require a new cast at the new fight position. An implicit area must not recruit an unengaged hostile or break protected CC inside its radius. This changes spell selection, not tank navigation/positioning.
- **Dispel policy:** selected native effect38 abilities run at priority42, after emergency protection and before HoT maintenance. Dispel types come from those effect rows. The picker shares ordinary dispel eligibility: non-passive harmful auras on allies, beneficial auras on hostiles, matching the actual native type (including native All). Filter eligible patients before health ranking; never spend a dispel on an empty or wrong-type patient. Description-only dummy wrappers without a native dispel effect remain inventoried utility, not synthetic dispel spells.
- **Caster-control policy:** sourced Polymorph/Hammer of Justice/Leg Sweep/Paralysis/Fear/Hex with native stopping-aura effects may control an engaged casting enemy at priority43, after emergency protection/dispels and before maintenance. Never chase for a control cast, re-control an already controlled target, manufacture an aura or assume a dummy/placement spell has a stopping effect. Source area radii and the existing fresh-pack/CC protection policy apply to area controls. Ordinary replacement validation must succeed before stopping the follower's cast; an already admitted identical control cast must finish. General CC tactics, roots/knockbacks/ground placements and full-kit coverage remain separate.
### Shared interrupt contract

The planner supplies ordered engaged-caster candidates; the picker alone validates and attempts ordinary interrupts in that order. Source-level `interrupt` rows precede learned attack rows with native interrupt effects, preserving Rebuke before Avenger's Shield. Never spend on a finished/uninterruptible/protected-CC target, chase an out-of-range candidate, retarget the follower or cancel its existing cast before a replacement validates. Continue to the next candidate when no source spell is ready. `true` means a normal cast was accepted, not a guaranteed hit. Absent spec rows retain the explicit legacy bridge; Demonology uses its actual learned pet interrupt, not an invented player spell. Ordinary source-authored Skull Bash106839 retains its parent, cost, range and charge221514; picker-authored movement/navigation is prohibited. Provider/caller activation and removal of old embedded interrupts must land atomically, with no interrupt-less runtime interval.
### Manual utility boundary

Sourced passive and noncombat/movement/explicit utility spells remain inventoried with `passive`/`utility` rows; the autonomous combat picker never teleports, swaps forms, resurrects, creates items or chooses an invented Hunter pet. Unsupported combat-effect behavior remains a spell-system gap, not permission to synthesize it. Inventory breadth is not proof that every spell is executable or optimal.
