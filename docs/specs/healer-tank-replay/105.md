# Restoration Druid healer replay

- Choose the most injured living party member, maintain owned Lifebloom and Rejuvenation, and use Swiftmend when injured and native admission permits. Use Wild Growth and Efflorescence for multiple injuries, selected Grove Guardians or Convoke, and Tranquility for emergencies. Use Moonfire and Wrath when no healing action is needed. This triage policy is not a SimC healer APL.
