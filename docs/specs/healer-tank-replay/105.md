# Restoration Druid healer replay

- Choose the most injured living party member, maintain owned Lifebloom and Rejuvenation (including a second Germination when selected), and use Swiftmend when injured and native admission permits. Use Wild Growth and Efflorescence for multiple injuries, selected Grove Guardians or Convoke, and Tranquility for emergencies. Use Moonfire and Wrath when no healing action is needed. This triage policy is not a SimC healer APL. When no healing action is due, the druid casts Rejuvenation on the lowest-health party member without full Rejuvenation coverage (including the Germination copy) before Moonfire or Wrath. WCL `kD8MqXLV6CFYHN2p`, fight 3, actor 108 applies Rejuvenation 24.5 times per minute (15.4 casts plus 9.1 Germination) and casts no Wrath. A party member counts as injured for these group heals below 94% health. Source: WCL `kD8MqXLV6CFYHN2p`, fight 3, actor 108 (key 17). At its 72 Wild Growth casts, the second-lowest party member has a median 93.9% health (q25 84.6%, q75 100%). Each member's health is the `hitPoints`/`maxHitPoints` of their latest heal event within 1.5 seconds before the cast (`wcl events --type Healing --hostility Friendlies --resources`).

## Grove Guardians

Selected Grove Guardians 1226140 makes each successful Swiftmend or Wild Growth summon Treant 54983 through spell 102693. Each treant lasts eight seconds and casts Nourish 422090 on the original target or a nearby ally. The two-second base cast uses the owner's haste; the heal uses the native spell-power coefficient and receives 100% of the owner's Harmony bonus for their HoTs on the recipient. The passive trigger is not a manually cast action and does not consume the legacy three-charge category on 102693. Native 70448 `Spell.csv` and `SpellEffect` specify these relationships; WCL `mR86tKbdCqF1A2NY`, fight 4, actor 4, shows summon events on the triggering casts and repeated 422090 heals from the spawned treants.

## Symbiotic Blooms

Thriving Growth 439528 is selected in the reference build. Eligible Wild Growth 48438, Regrowth 8936 and Efflorescence 81269 healing can apply independently expiring Symbiotic Blooms 439530 to the recipient. SimC `76f8118` explicitly marks its Symbiotic Blooms RNG NYI. The 70448 aura options have no RPPM and use ProcChance 101; they do not specify a usable healing proc rate.

Until a stronger source resolves the algorithm, use the observed rate 257/7156 (3.59%) per eligible heal. The sample is WCL report `mR86tKbdCqF1A2NY`, fight 4, actor 4: exclude eligible heals within 100 ms of Swiftmend or Wild Growth casts, and exclude same-target eligible heals with another eligible heal within 200 ms. Count a success when the same target has a 439530 `applybuff` or `applybuffstack` within ±100 ms. The 95% Wilson interval is 3.18–4.05%; it covers sampling uncertainty only. Delayed logs and coincident effects can cause missed or false matches. The selected Wild Growth/Swiftmend talent 440118 supplies a separate application on a valid cast target, with its native six-second duration. Revisit the empirical proc when source-specific rates or attribution are available.
