# Protection Warrior 73 tank replay

The Patchwerk DPS reference and native DPS fixture are not a like-for-like intake scenario. The pinned reference has an attacking `Fluffy_Pillow` with melee, melee nuke, bleed and tank healing; mean damage taken is 8.09M. The native DPS fixture uses passive dummy 31146 and sampled health remains full. Their talent strings, equipped item IDs and pinned profile SHA match; the native literal APL comes from that profile. Therefore the observed DPS gap is not by itself a parity result.

The SimC dungeon-dummy scenario uses a level-92 attacker, difficulty 23, content tuning 3025 and MythicPlusSeason 120. ExpectedStatMod 448 supplies its armor coefficient; older measurements below predate this scenario correction.

- The MID2 profile selects Pain and Gain (382549). SimC `76f8118` declares and looks up the talent but does not implement its heal. Two Midnight Magisters' Terrace Protection Warrior logs show that 382551 heals self for 2.3278% of current maximum health in the observed conditions: report `4qJHzQpV9kxC8Dvd`, fight 3, actor 6, pull 7 (16 heals in 266.7 s; 12,007 / 515,794 and 14,048 / 603,478), and report `bxaz2dYWHmkMRTCq`, fight 36, actor 5, pull 5 (16 heals in 267.4 s; 12,006 / 515,794). These values include any live healing modifiers. The native effect's 20 points previously healed ten times too much; native scales the 382551 result by 0.1 to match the observed health fraction. The logs also show approximately 10-second minimum spacing; the observed per-minute rate depends on incoming hits.
- Selected Fueled by Violence (383103) heals the warrior through 383104 from actual Rend and Deep Wounds periodic damage.
- Sudden Death's RPPM and cooldown are owned by its foreground special-execution policy; background hits do not spend its proc window.
- Avatar's Thunder Clap damage and cooldown modifiers require selected Storm Surge; this Colossus profile does not select it.
- Selected Thunderlord reduces Demoralizing Shout recovery for Thunder Clap and Thunder Blast's selected enemies, including fully absorbed hits, up to its native target cap.
- Selected Frothing Berserker can refund part of the Rage actually paid for Revenge; a free Revenge has no Rage to refund.
- Revenge Trigger (5301) rolls on outgoing white impacts and incoming dodges or parries. Selected Best Served Cold (202560) increases its RPPM by 20% through native `SpellProcsPerMinuteMod` type 8.
- Into the Fray (202603) grants native 202602 haste stacks from actual nearby allies and enemies. The pinned SimC defaults to four stacks even in a single-target fixture (`sc_warrior.cpp:9042, 9116–9119`), so that scenario includes assumed nearby units absent from the native replay.
- Selected Blood and Thunder (384277) applies Rend (388539) on landed Thunder Clap and Thunder Blast hits.
- War Machine uses its Protection-specific auto-attack Rage modifier, separately from the Protection passive's modifier.
- Ravager grants the native 334934 Rage payload once per pulse with at least one target; extra targets do not multiply it.
- Selected Celeritous Conclusion grants its native haste and Shield Slam critical-chance buffs on Demolish's final strike; Shield Slam consumes the critical-chance buff.
- Brace for Impact 386029 increases shield block value by its native second effect per active stack. The modifier applies before the attacker's armor-constant block formula for swings and blockable spells (`sc_warrior.cpp:9292–9302`). Warrior mastery critical blocks are capped at 85% of the incoming pre-block damage (`sc_warrior.cpp:9517–9522`, `util.cpp:3582–3589`).
- Punish 275335 reduces the afflicted attacker's damage to the casting warrior by its native effect per stack, for melee, direct spells and bleeds (`sc_warrior.cpp:9638`). Aura 269 is SimC's caster-scoped `A_MOD_DAMAGE_TO_CASTER` for every spell (581.md), not resistance bypass.
- Selected Violent Outburst (386477) tracks actual paid Rage. At its native threshold it grants 386478; Shield Slam consumes the buff and grants native Ignore Pain.
- Demolish executes its three damage spells during the channel. Selected Decimator applies Deep Wounds on the final hit. Selected Deep Wounds uses rolling refreshes that preserve the next periodic tick. Foreground Demolish and Demoralizing Shout also roll selected Sudden Death.
- Shield Block has one base charge in category 1385 and passive 231847 adds one. Native retains two charges. Pinned SimC applies that charge twice: the debug trace changes 2565's charges from one to two via 231847, `action.cpp:709–714` initializes from the modified spell, and `sc_warrior.cpp:7187–7188` adds the passive again. The trace shows three charges (`2/3` after the cast at 1.585 s, `1/3` at 7.244 s). This is a SimC double-application bug; do not copy it. Rank-2 Enduring Defenses adds two seconds to each Shield Block duration; Heavy Repercussions, which extends it on Shield Slam, is not selected. Native charge recovery scales with haste and retimes when haste changes.
- SimC dynamically retimes ordinary Shield Slam cooldowns when haste changes. Native scales its cooldown at cast time; its ordinary cooldown has no corresponding dynamic retime. In the canonical SimC trace the observed in-flight adjustments have a net slowing effect, not an established cause of the native cast deficit.
