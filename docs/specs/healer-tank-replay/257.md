# Holy Priest (257) healer replay

The replay uses a native healing priority, not a SimC healer APL: SimC `76f8118` supplies no Priest healer APL. The policy selects the lowest-health living party member, casts Flash Heal when the target is missing at least 80% of its expected healing, uses Prayer of Mending and Halo when available, and otherwise fills with Holy Fire or Smite. It reserves defensive and group cooldowns for low health.

The fixture checks that each reaches the tank and pays its native mana cost.
