# Holy Priest (257) healer replay

The replay uses a native healing priority, not the experimental SimC healer APL. SimC `76f8118` contains a Holy APL in `engine/class_modules/apl/apl_priest.cpp:259–345`, but its Priest healer role is explicitly unsupported in `sc_priest.cpp:3805–3816`; that APL is damage-oriented. The policy selects the lowest-health living party member, uses Serenity or Flash Heal when the target is missing at least 80% of Flash Heal's expected healing, uses Prayer of Mending and Halo when available, and otherwise fills with Holy Fire or Smite. It reserves defensive and group cooldowns for low health.

The MID2 profile does not select Renew or Halo; Heal 2060 is not in its native spellbook. The native target-and-cost test grants Renew and Circle of Healing only in its fixture; Serenity and Flash Heal are exercised with the supplied profile. The fixture checks that each reaches the tank and pays its native mana cost.

## Reference and open gaps (2026-10-29)

The top-character profile with the default experimental APL fails to initialize `divine_star`. SimC registers Holy Fire, Apotheosis, Chastise, Serenity, Sanctify and Guardian Spirit as Holy actions and shared Flash Heal, Smite, Shield, Halo and Holy Nova. Echo of Light is implemented as a background action. Renew has a class implementation but no action registration; Prayer of Mending, Heal, Circle of Healing and Divine Hymn are not registered by the Priest action factories. Registration alone does not prove correct healing.

### SimC double-application bug: Echo of Light crit

SimC `sc_priest.hpp:1463–1474` adds `result_total`, which already includes the originating direct heal's critical result, to the Echo residual pool. `action/residual_action.hpp:48–59` leaves periodic critical rolls enabled. The custom five-recipient run reports 66 critical Echo ticks out of 247. The WCL reference reports zero critical Echo ticks among 3,304 ticks. Thus a critical originating heal can be multiplied by critical strike again when its pooled Echo ticks crit. Treat that double application as a SimC bug, not a native parity requirement; the aggregate run does not isolate the size of this effect or prove the complete native Echo model. Native Echo ticks use their already-resolved residual amount without reapplying healing modifiers or rolling crit.

Open: no same-input SimC healer comparison or literal native SimC APL; no WCL-to-replay pressure equivalence; no first-divergence comparison or 258 impact measurement.
