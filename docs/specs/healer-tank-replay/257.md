# Holy Priest (257) healer replay

The replay uses a native healing priority, not the experimental SimC healer APL. SimC `76f8118` contains a Holy APL in `engine/class_modules/apl/apl_priest.cpp:259–345`, but its Priest healer role is explicitly unsupported in `sc_priest.cpp:3805–3816`; that APL is damage-oriented. The policy selects the lowest-health living party member, casts Flash Heal when the target is missing at least 80% of its expected healing, uses Prayer of Mending and Halo when available, and otherwise fills with Holy Fire or Smite. It reserves defensive and group cooldowns for low health.

The MID2 profile does not select Renew or Halo; Heal 2060 is not in its native spellbook. The native target-and-cost test grants Renew and Circle of Healing only in its fixture; Serenity and Flash Heal are exercised with the supplied profile. The fixture checks that each reaches the tank and pays its native mana cost.

## Reference and open gaps (2026-10-29)

The top-character profile with the default experimental APL fails to initialize `divine_star`. SimC registers Holy Fire, Apotheosis, Chastise, Serenity, Sanctify and Guardian Spirit as Holy actions and shared Flash Heal, Smite, Shield, Halo and Holy Nova. Echo of Light is implemented as a background action. Renew has a class implementation but no action registration; Prayer of Mending, Heal, Circle of Healing and Divine Hymn are not registered by the Priest action factories. Registration alone does not prove correct healing.

A bounded SimC substitute ran seed 70448 for 300 seconds with `healing=4`, the 20,000 Shadow pulse at 8 seconds every 12 seconds, and the custom policy `holy_word_serenity,target=Healing_Target_1,if=target.health.pct<90/flash_heal,target=Healing_Target_1,if=target.health.pct<90/smite`. It produced 38 Serenity, 120 Flash Heal and 111 Smite casts and 81,218.30 HPS. This uses synthetic healing targets and SimC's own tank-dummy attacks, not the native recorded tank trace or the same health model; it is not a parity comparison. Source artifacts: `game-engine/data/diagnostics/healer-tank-replay/priest-reference/257.custom-five.{json,log}`.
