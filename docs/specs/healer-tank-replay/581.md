# Vengeance Demon Hunter 581 replay

Fiery Brand's successful impact applies its native 207771 periodic debuff to the target and its defensive effect to the caster. The inactive historical tier effect is excluded, as in pinned SimC.

Selected Charred Flesh extends the caster's Fiery Brand and Sigil of Flame target effects on each landed Immolation Aura hit, using the selected talent rank's native duration. The equipped MID2 two-piece bonus extends the caster's Sigil of Flame on each landed Soul Cleave damage hit. The equipped four-piece bonus increases Immolation Aura and Sigil of Spite damage against targets affected by the caster's Sigil of Flame.

Selected Infernal Armor damages hostile attackers that land direct physical hits while Immolation Aura is active. Selected Retaliation damages attackers that land melee swings while Demon Spikes is active.

Selected Fallout generates lesser soul fragments from actual Immolation Aura initial hits. The initial hit is guaranteed to generate one fragment against a single target; each hit in a multi-target cast rolls the pinned SimC chance. Untethered Rage modifies the native fragment caps of Spirit Bomb and Soul Cleave and Spirit Bomb's damage per consumed fragment.

Selected Focused Cleave increases Soul Cleave damage only on its primary target by the native talent value.

Sigil of Flame grants Vengeance its native 389787 Fury payload on cast (SimC76f8118 `sc_demon_hunter.cpp:4469–4473,11262`). Metamorphosis grants Fracture its native extra Fury without requiring rank 321068 (SimC76f8118 `sc_demon_hunter.cpp:8069–8078`). Felblade executes its native damage and Fury child for Vengeance, as it does for Havoc. Selected Mass Acceleration resets Spirit Bomb and generates Voidfall stacks when Metamorphosis is cast. Fracture can generate Voidfall stacks; spending them schedules the selected meteor actions. Scheduled meteors still execute if overlapping spenders exhaust the remaining stacks before their launch; World Killer reduces the Metamorphosis cooldown on impact.

The MID2 profile selects Live by the Glaive (428607); its native healing is reported as 428608. It also selects Charred Warblades (213010), which accumulates eligible Fire damage and heals through 213011. Pinned SimC marks both talents as not implemented (`sc_demon_hunter.cpp:439,454`), so neither heal has a SimC equivalent. Consume Soul 203794 is a separate healing source.

Selected Vulnerability makes the caster's damage against Frailty targets increase per stack, using the selected Vulnerability and Soulcrush native values.

Frailty applies on successful Sigil of Flame, Soul Cleave damage and Spirit Bomb damage. Damage dealt to a target with the caster's Frailty accumulates healing from the debuff's per-stack native value; spell 227255 delivers the accumulated healing once per second.

Leech uses the equipped rating and Soul Rending's native effects, including its Metamorphosis modifier. Spirit Bomb, Soul Cleave, Sigil of Flame and Fiery Brand periodic damage feed the generic leech pool.

Selected Otherworldly Focus increases Spirit Bomb, Voidfall Meteor, World Killer and Meteor Shower damage, with the native bonus reduced for each additional area recipient. Selected Catastrophe rolls a fraction of actual Voidfall Meteor, World Killer and Meteor Shower damage into periodic damage on the struck target.

Selected Dark Matter is earned by Metamorphosis and consumed by Spirit Bomb's delayed damage action. It creates a Meteor Shower at the target's position with the native number of damage pulses.
