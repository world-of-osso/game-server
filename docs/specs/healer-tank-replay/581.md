# Vengeance Demon Hunter 581 replay

Fiery Brand's successful impact applies its native 207771 periodic debuff to the target and its defensive effect to the caster. The inactive historical tier effect is excluded, as in pinned SimC.

Selected Fallout generates lesser soul fragments from actual Immolation Aura initial hits. The initial hit is guaranteed to generate one fragment against a single target; each hit in a multi-target cast rolls the pinned SimC chance.

Felblade executes its native damage and Fury child for Vengeance, as it does for Havoc. Selected Mass Acceleration resets Spirit Bomb and generates Voidfall stacks when Metamorphosis is cast. Fracture can generate Voidfall stacks; spending them triggers the selected meteor actions.

The MID2 profile selects Live by the Glaive (428607); its native healing is reported as 428608. Pinned SimC marks this talent as not implemented (`sc_demon_hunter.cpp:454`), so its native healing has no SimC equivalent. Consume Soul 203794 is a separate healing source.

Frailty applies on successful Sigil of Flame, Soul Cleave damage and Spirit Bomb damage. Damage dealt to a target with the caster's Frailty accumulates healing from the debuff's per-stack native value; spell 227255 delivers the accumulated healing once per second.

Leech uses the equipped rating and Soul Rending's native effects, including its Metamorphosis modifier. Spirit Bomb, Soul Cleave, Sigil of Flame and Fiery Brand periodic damage feed the generic leech pool.

Selected Catastrophe rolls a fraction of actual Voidfall Meteor, World Killer and Meteor Shower damage into periodic damage on the struck target.

Selected Dark Matter is earned by Metamorphosis and consumed by Spirit Bomb's delayed damage action. It creates a Meteor Shower at the target's position with the native number of damage pulses.
