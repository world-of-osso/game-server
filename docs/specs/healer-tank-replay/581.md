### Armor coefficient qualification

Native combat uses `min(armor / (armor + K), 0.85)` and truncates the resulting damage. `UnitScaling::armor_constant` uses the attacker's effective level and its content-tuning multiplier. SimC `util.cpp:3582–3588` uses the same ratio and cap; its tank dummy obtains K from the maximum player level and difficulty (`sc_enemy.cpp:1214–1237,2097–2157`).

**VERIFIED against DB2 70448 and the recorded SimC traces, not independently against WCL:** snapshot v3 `ExpectedStat` has base K 793 at level 70, 813 at 72, and 3,430 at both 90 and 92. The level-70 and level-90 class-follower traces above explicitly set the attacker to the player's level and record K 793 and 3,430, respectively; they are not the separate dungeon-scenario replays. For content tuning 3025 and MythicPlusSeason 120, `ContentTuningXExpected` selects ExpectedStatMod 448, whose armor multiplier is 1.32099998. Thus level 92 dungeon K is 4,531.03 on both sides. At level 70, a level-72 dungeon attacker would instead use native K 1,073.97, while SimC's level-70 dummy lookup gives 1,047.55. These are different level-selection models, not evidence that either value applies to the recorded level-70 class-follower trace. WCL samples above support the armor changes but do not independently isolate raw incoming damage and K; they cannot verify the K formula or decide that level-72 difference. No data override is introduced by this comparison.

### Outgoing auto-attack avoidance scenario gap

Seed 70448 produced 24 blocked and two parried native white swings; the SimC trace has neither. Native resolves the actual position and creature avoidance (`server-combat/src/combat.rs:1735–1768`); SimC's white-attack table has no block outcome (`attack.cpp:189–285`). The pinned profile and initial stats explicitly report `position=front` (trace lines 654 and 2179), so a behind-target replay override would not match that recorded input. The live creature parry base is 6% plus 1.5 points per higher level (`melee.rs`), while SimC's enemy base is 3% plus level adjustment (`attack.cpp`, `player.cpp`); SimC applies player expertise before its outcome roll. These are unresolved outcome-model and scenario differences; no shared creature table or replay position is changed here.

### Known SimC Final Hour double count

Final Hour 1253805 says each consumed Voidfall stack retains its passive bonuses for eight seconds. Native consumes three spending stacks and retains three Final Hour stacks. Pinned SimC `sc_demon_hunter.cpp:9354–9379` grants the last stack once on expiration and again after decrement; `buff.cpp:2246–2256` calls expiration when decrement consumes that last stack. Three stacks therefore become four in SimC. SimC also grants one when a delayed meteor calls decrement after spending is already empty. Neither phantom grant is a native parity target; native Final Hour only follows real consumption. The extra stack supplies 2% haste and 2 percentage points of damage-taken reduction while active when Swift Erasure and Phase Shift are selected. Native effects and descriptions: `SpellDataDump/demonhunter.txt:15612–15625,15896–15925`.

A local counterfactual copy of `76f8118` removed only the duplicate last-stack grant; it is not the reference. With the same profile, options and seed, ten measured iterations changed Demon Spikes uptime from 91.4929% to 90.5548% and damage taken from 5,949,784 to 6,114,991. Both runs had zero deaths. The change affects future random and rotation trajectories, so these whole-run differences are not a fixed-hit causal decomposition. The patched source and output are in `/home/osso/.worktrees/logs/simc-581-no-extra-finalhour/`.

## Damage-taken decomposition and aura 269

Aura 269 is SimC's `A_MOD_DAMAGE_TO_CASTER` (`parse_effects.cpp:1148–1159, 1468–1471`): it scales only damage the aura holder deals to that aura's caster, by school mask. Native 70448 tooltips agree ("Dealing $w3% reduced damage to $@auracaster"). Native had read it as TC's ignore-resist bypass. Frailty 247456 effect 2 applies only with selected Void Reaver (`sc_demon_hunter.cpp:12743–12746`); selected Soulcrush doubles it to −12%.

On seed 70448, a native diagnostic (master `034e329723`) and a single SimC iteration were split by event type. Each had 148 swings, 10 strikes and 150 bleed ticks. Per landed swing before absorbs, native took 34,717 and SimC 33,782. Armor, versatility, Fiery Brand uptime (30.5% / 28.0%) and parry chance (23.6% / 21.4%) were close. After removing armor and versatility, the residual multiplier was 0.660 / 0.605 for swings and 0.652 / 0.609 for bleeds. The difference matched Frailty (native about 0.96 from the bypass formula, SimC 0.88).

After the aura-269 fix (`865db2502e`), healed seeds 70448–70450 took 5,935,050, 5,679,104 and 6,079,546 damage (mean 5,897,900). The SimC references are ten-measured-iteration means:

| SimC reference | Damage taken | Native vs reference |
|---|---:|---:|
| Canonical `76f8118` | 5,949,784 | −0.9% |
| Immolation Aura armor double application removed | 6,253,454 | −5.7% |
| Double application removed, Demon Spikes armor added | 5,812,546 | +1.5% |

- **Double application:** SimC applies Immolation Aura's +20% armor twice: once through `parse_effects`, and again in `composite_armor_multiplier` (`sc_demon_hunter.cpp:11974–11984`). Removing the second application raises SimC's armor by about 2,512 on affected swings.
- **Omission:** SimC omits Demon Spikes 203819 effect 2 (`A_MOD_ARMOR_PCT_FROM_STAT`, 75% of Agility). Native data and the 203720 tooltip include it. SimC's `composite_armor` models only Fel Blood's doubling. Native keeps the 70448 effect.

The corrected reference applies the missing armor flat, after multipliers, as SimC does for Fel Blood. Native adds stat-derived armor before total-armor multipliers, as in TC `UpdateArmor`. That ordering difference is not attributed. The counterfactual copy is `/home/osso/.worktrees/simc-tank73-noarmor`; its 581 outputs are `581.counterfactual-*-10measured.json` in the diagnostics directory. Three seeds are not ten-seed parity proof.
