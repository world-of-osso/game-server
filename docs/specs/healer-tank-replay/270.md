- The pinned SimC default action lists do not include Mistweaver. The replay uses native readiness and party health to select healing actions. Its damage priority uses Spinning Crane Kick when at least two living enemies are within its native 8-yard pulse radius; a one-enemy party pulse should not cast it. WCL report `6Lg4hCVtj12JNbXc` fight 41 actor 730 uses it 6.94 times/min in a multi-pull dungeon.
- Ancient Teachings 388023 transfers eligible Monk damage among up to five injured allies within the native 40-yard radius. Its 70448 base is 25%; selected Jadefire Teachings 467293 modifies that value by 320 percentage points through spell label 2577. The healing preserves the damage hit's critical result and uses 388024 for noncritical or 388025 for critical hits.
- Renewing Mist requires a health deficit. When Vivify resolves to the group heal Sheilun's Gift, its heal-worthwhile gate uses the total living-party deficit; single-target Vivify uses its target's deficit. When no healing action is admitted, the policy uses Rising Sun Kick, Blackout Kick or Tiger Palm against the tank's enemy.
