# Mistweaver Monk replay

- The pinned SimC default action lists do not include Mistweaver. The replay uses native readiness and party health to select healing actions, including selected Chi-Ji when the target is below 90% health. Its damage priority uses Spinning Crane Kick when at least two living enemies are within its native 8-yard pulse radius; a one-enemy party pulse should not cast it. WCL report `6Lg4hCVtj12JNbXc` fight 41 actor 730 uses it 6.94 times/min in a multi-pull dungeon.
- Mastery 117907 triggers Gust of Mists 191894 on the primary target of Renewing Mist and Enveloping Mist. Its healing scales with mastery through the native spell-power coefficient modifier.
- Invoke Chi-Ji 325197 summons creature 166949 for 25 seconds. While active, Blackout Kick, Rising Sun Kick and Spinning Crane Kick trigger three 343819 Gusts on each of two party members, at 50% of the native amount. Rushing Wind Kick 467307 counts as Rising Sun Kick: it replaces it and carries its class flag (SpellClassMask_1 128); 343818 has no proc mask and neither WCL reference took the talent. The tooltip says "nearest", but in WCL `TQXmJ6YVr71Pdycw` fight 8 actor 837 the two recipients match the monk-nearest pair in 3 of 86 kicks and the pair nearest Chi-Ji's melee target in 10 of 88, while all five players receive Gusts. Gusts therefore choose randomly among party members, injured first, excluding summons. While Chi-Ji is active and no ally is below 35%, the policy kicks before healing: the WCL actors average 10–18 kicks and under two heals per window. The 70448 Invoke effects make Enveloping Mist instant and reduce its mana cost by 20%; the older stacking description on 331645/358522 is not the current 325197 tooltip. TrinityCore's monk scripts contain no Invoke Chi-Ji implementation.
