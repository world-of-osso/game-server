# Mistweaver Monk replay

- The pinned SimC default action lists do not include Mistweaver. The replay uses native readiness and party health to select healing actions, including selected Chi-Ji when the target is below 90% health. Its damage priority uses Spinning Crane Kick when at least two living enemies are within its native 8-yard pulse radius; a one-enemy party pulse should not cast it. WCL report `6Lg4hCVtj12JNbXc` fight 41 actor 730 uses it 6.94 times/min in a multi-pull dungeon.
- Mastery 117907 triggers Gust of Mists 191894 on the primary target of Renewing Mist and Enveloping Mist. Its healing scales with mastery through the native spell-power coefficient modifier.
- Invoke Chi-Ji 325197 summons creature 166949 for 25 seconds. While active, Blackout Kick, Rising Sun Kick and Spinning Crane Kick trigger three 343819 Gusts on each of two party members, at 50% of the native amount. Rushing Wind Kick 467307 counts as Rising Sun Kick: it replaces it and carries its class flag (SpellClassMask_1 128); 343818 has no proc mask and neither WCL reference took the talent. The tooltip says "nearest", but in WCL `TQXmJ6YVr71Pdycw` fight 8 actor 837 the two recipients match the monk-nearest pair in 3 of 86 kicks and the pair nearest Chi-Ji's melee target in 10 of 88, while all five players receive Gusts. With two to four members injured, both recipients were injured in 20 of 32 kicks against 11.6 expected at random. Gusts therefore choose randomly among party members, injured first, excluding summons. While Chi-Ji is active and no ally is below 35%, the policy kicks before healing: the WCL actors average 10–18 kicks and under two heals per window. The 70448 Invoke effects make Enveloping Mist instant and reduce its mana cost by 20%; the older stacking description on 331645/358522 is not the current 325197 tooltip. TrinityCore's monk scripts contain no Invoke Chi-Ji implementation.
- Celestial Harmony 343655: invoking Chi-Ji or Yu'lon casts Chi Cocoon 406220 on up to 406139 s3 (5) party members within 40 yards, injured first, excluding summons and pets. In WCL `6Lg4hCVtj12JNbXc` fight 41 actor 730, 102 of 105 406220 applications follow a Chi-Ji summon, five per summon. Its 70448 base is 25%; selected Jadefire Teachings 467293 modifies that value by 320 percentage points through spell label 2577. When Vivify resolves to the group heal Sheilun's Gift, its heal-worthwhile gate uses the total living-party deficit; single-target Vivify uses its target's deficit. When no healing action is admitted, the policy uses Rising Sun Kick, Blackout Kick or Tiger Palm against the tank's enemy.
