# Raw WCL tank replay

## Contract

`scripts/healer_wcl_intake.py --mode tank` emits `mode: tank_raw` for the configured first actor. The default and explicit `--mode healer` preserve the existing byte-level output contract. Tank gear and talents come from the five recorded CombatantInfo builds. No MID2 profile changes are permitted.

Raw receipts retain time, school, defense type, source actor and instance, ability, periodic flag, logged outcome, unmitigated amount, recorded loss/absorb and time-varying max-health observation. Positive hits without raw amounts, friendly redirects, and hits where raw differs from loss plus absorption plus mitigation are excluded with counted raw/observed-pressure receipts. Unknown raw amounts must not be represented as a known excluded-raw percentage. Self damage is not replayed as hostile pressure; native self damage remains native.

The native replay preserves logged miss/dodge/parry/immune and block/critical classifications. It does not infer unavailable raw rolls for avoided events or roll avoidance again. Native armor, damage-taken modifiers, block amount (including native critical-block chance), Stagger, shields, transfers and hostile-hit procs resolve through the existing melee/spell receipt paths. Raw crit amounts already include the logged critical result; they must not be doubled again. Raw pressure is normalized by observed victim max HP to native victim max HP, then divided by native source-to-HP scaling before resolution. Recorded critical results remain available to procs.

Each enemy profile interval must have a matching `source_keys` entry `[sourceActor, sourceInstance]`. Raw hits must map to an active native enemy with that key: a branded enemy must not stand in for all attackers. Intervals inferred from first/last damage should be rounded outward to the native 50 ms frame. They do not prove exact spawn/despawn times. Native dummy levels remain scenario inputs, not observed WCL NPC levels.

Non-self logged effective healing onto the tank is replayed in chronological order with incoming hits, with damage first on tied milliseconds. Healing is normalized to native max HP, without another healing bonus or critical roll. Native overheal and heal absorption still apply. `pet_owners` in config explicitly maps WCL summons to party-owner slots; unknown owners fail, not silently map to the healer. Non-tank APLs are held during tank replay. A replay-only external-heal gate also blocks their native direct heals, opening HoT ticks and healing procs onto the tank. Each logged input grants one matching source/spell permission, consumed before heal/proc dispatch; native tank self-healing remains active. Suppressed native external-heal attempts are counted by spell in the report. This gate is absent from normal gameplay and healer-mode fixtures. Opening auras preserve the existing duration/source limitations; external shields or later external defensive buffs are not inferred from healing amounts.

## Logged external shields and opening balances

In tank mode, non-tank-source Buffs apply/refresh events with a logged `absorb` amount and native SCHOOL_ABSORB metadata become timed `external_shields` receipts. Grants are normalized to native max HP and replace the owned pool with the recorded absolute value; refresh is not another deposit. Shield grants precede tied-millisecond damage, and damage precedes tied healing. Native armor/DR and shield consumption still run. A fixture-only permission gate blocks independent native external shield grants, with counted attempts, while preserving native tank-owned shields. External consumption receipts are reported separately from self shields. No healer or tank cast-choice policy changes.

An opening external static shield may be reconstructed as consumed points plus the explicit remaining `absorb` field on its first removal. The native spell must have a shield and no periodic effects, with no earlier in-pull refresh. These balances are labelled `INFERRED_WCL_static_shield_receipts_and_remainder`. Unknown opening external shield budgets fail explicitly. No starting pool is inferred for decaying shields. A nonzero recorded removal remainder schedules the observed removal; a zero remainder represents consumption, so native hits spend that pool instead of forcing its removal at the logged depletion time.

For tank-owned static potion shield 1235568, reconstruction additionally requires a final absorbed receipt with a matching partially damaging hit. Other tank-owned shields are not reconstructed by this rule. Restored points already include the logged modifiers and do not receive outgoing shield bonuses a second time.

For Warrior `CZ6jq9Q8DJHapMBc` f24 p1, 1235568 absorbs 88,947 and 43,707 at pull 1.163/1.164 s; the final hit also loses 43,312 HP. The restored 132,654 opening points are inferred remaining state, not a potion coefficient or quality. The pre-pull 30-second Buffs/Casts query and the full-key potion Casts query contain no application/use event, and CombatantInfo does not identify a quality. The DB2 spell is shared by several item effects; no production potion defect or automatic potion choice follows from this reconstruction.

## Evidence and limits

The report retains native heal/absorb spell receipts, per-hit health loss, tank cast timing and five-second mitigation frame counts for Shield Block (73/132404), Demon Spikes (581/203819), Shield of the Righteous (66/132403), Shuffle (268/215479), Ironfur (104/192081), or Bone Shield (250/195181). The report identifies the tracked aura; these are presence/absence counts, not stack-weighted mitigation. Self-heal receipts include frame-end maximum health for normalization. This is a 50 ms simulation, not sub-frame event timing. WCL maximum health observations and opening aura remaining duration are not exact state snapshots. Excluded damage, unresolved external defenses and source scaling must accompany comparisons. Observed cast timing is not a new cooldown trigger policy.

## Verification

- Python tests compare default/explicit healer CLI output to a golden byte fixture generated before tank mode, and exercise raw inclusion, logged avoidance, excluded raw and non-self healing.
- Native raw-hit fixture exercises actual Stagger deposition; the healer fixture proves post-mitigation pressure still bypasses Stagger.
- Logged external-shield fixture exercises actual grant absorption, absolute refresh and rejection of duplicate native grants; Python tests cover external timing and static remainder reconstruction without guessing a decaying pool.
- Logged external-heal fixture first observes an actual native Renew tick, then proves the persistent HoT is blocked under the raw replay gate. It proves effective amount normalization, one-use permission, preserved native self-healing and refusal to resurrect a dead tank. The earlier APL-only approach duplicated Earthliving: in the sampled Warrior pull the log supplies 32 events but native produced 49, and Vengeance supplies 28 versus 42.
- Per-pull diagnostic reports are evidence, not general tank parity claims. Policy changes require ready-but-not-cast separation from at least 50 casts by several players; medians at cast are not triggers.
