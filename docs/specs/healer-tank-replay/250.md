# Blood Death Knight tank replay

The MID2 profile's `draught_of_rampant_abandon_2` uses native quality-2 item `241292` (item level 295) through the shared literal item-use path. This follows the existing 66/73 Liquid Luster fixture precedent in `profile_effects.rs` and `literal_rotation/execution.rs`; the same mapping also supplies the 268 profile. Earlier native measurements below did not include this potion, while their SimC references did. They do not isolate the remaining AP gap.

A three-seed diagnostic with the native potion showed 9.983% potion uptime in each run, versus 10.00% in the pinned single SimC trace. Native Death Strike impacts had mean AP 5,229 / 5,251 / 5,180 for seeds 70448–70450 (pooled 5,220); the independent single SimC trace had mean AP 5,870 over 101 impacts. This is not a matched or ten-seed comparison and does not establish the remaining AP gap's cause.

A later one-seed native stat snapshot found no Visceral Strength (`461130`), while the ten-iteration SimC reference reports 75.06% Visceral Strength grants +6% Strength. SimC `sc_death_knight.cpp:9717–9723` triggers it when Death and Decay consumes Crimson Scourge. The native producer and free-versus-paid cast test landed in `2f75223c1`. This explains part of the AP gap, not necessarily all of it.

The same native snapshot had no Bloodied Blade Strength stacks (`460499`) or final buff (`460500`), whereas the pinned SimC trace has 60 stack gains and six final gains. SimC `sc_death_knight.cpp:15990–16000,16685–16696` gives parries stacking Strength and replaces maximum stacks on the next parry with a final Strength buff and a strike. The native producer and actual-parry tests have landed.

The native snapshot also had no Rune Mastery (`374585`); SimC's ten-run reference reports 54.45% uptime at +6% Strength. SimC `sc_death_knight.cpp:13081–13098,15831–15833` rolls once per rune-cost action, including free casts. The landed native Blood payment hook covers foreground and free or costed triggered casts; a native Blood test verifies the selected rank's +6% Strength.

Blood-soaked Ground (`434034`) was also absent despite its selected talent. SimC `sc_death_knight.cpp:7451–7470,13835–13843` activates it on Death and Decay and adds the talent's effect 2 to Vampiric Strike proc chance. The pinned trace registers its native effect 1 as an 8% damage-taken reduction. Native field lifecycle, proc chance and incoming-strike tests have landed.

Ossuary (`219788`) follows the native Bone Shield stack threshold and provides its native Death Strike cost modifier. SimC uses the Blood-specific Improved Death Strike reduction, rather than the general reduction, before applying Ossuary (sc_death_knight.cpp:10064–10105,15187–15189,15972–15979). Their combined normal cost is 35 RP; without Ossuary it is 40 RP.

The SimC dungeon-dummy scenario uses a level-92 attacker, difficulty 23, content tuning 3025 and MythicPlusSeason 120. ExpectedStatMod 448 supplies its armor coefficient; older measurements below and the AP diagnostic predate this scenario correction.

## Known reference difference

Blood Plague follows SimC `76f8118` `sc_death_knight.cpp:6992–7037,7864–7872`: each damaging tick triggers a direct heal, multiplied by Rapid Decomposition and with an independent heal critical. This differs from native periodic leech's 1.25 amplitude. Selected Inevitable scales its damage with the target's missing health, reaching the native effect 2 percentage at 90% missing (SimC `sc_death_knight.cpp:7801–7812,7825–7830`).

- [ ] SimC `76f8118` applies the critical bonus twice to Vampiric Strike's percentage heal: the canonical trace shows 21,898.76 noncritical versus 91,169.62 critical, with two critical-bonus calculations. Native healing uses its normal single critical roll; the trace alone does not establish that the double application is real gameplay.
- [ ] SimC `76f8118` double-counts the same Hemostasis modifier for Death Strike healing: `sc_death_knight.cpp:16954` parses it, and `:10001` applies its stack value again. The canonical `spell_effect` row for spell `273947`, ID `725974`, index 0 is its only effect: aura 108, direct-amount modifier +8%. The pinned `SpellDataDump/deathknight.txt:6658–6664` lists both Death Strike heal `45470` and damage `49998` as affected. These are not two separate heal effects. Native applies the modifier once; SimC's double application also increases its Blood Shield.
