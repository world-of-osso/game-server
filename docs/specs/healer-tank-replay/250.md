# Blood Death Knight tank replay

## Permafrost

- Landed auto-attacks grant Frost Shield based on the Permafrost talent's damage percentage. Incoming damage consumes the shield; a new deposit replaces the remaining shield.

## Health-triggered defenses

Emergency Failsafe triggers below its authored health threshold and cannot recur while Recently Failed is active. Rune of Sanguination triggers below its authored health threshold and cannot recur while Satiated is active. The pinned spell descriptions specify these thresholds; SimC `76f8118` `sc_death_knight.cpp:12982–12987` instead checks for health above the Sanguination threshold. Its early Rune healing is therefore not the gameplay reference for this trigger.

## Known reference difference

- [ ] SimC `76f8118` applies Hemostasis twice to Death Strike healing (parsed action modifier and `death_strike_heal_t::action_multiplier`). Its debug trace shows `1.08²` for one 8% stack, about 8% more than one application. The source does not identify this as an in-game behavior; native gameplay applies it once. Blood Shield inherits the raw-heal difference.
