- Merithra's Blessing upgrades the next Reversion. Its override ends when the upgraded cast succeeds; only native Essence-cost abilities can trigger the upgrade.
- The pinned SimC Preservation default action list is empty; the replay uses a native-readiness healing policy.
- The policy uses Echo before major heals, Dream Breath on cooldown when a party member is below 95% health, and Verdant Embrace, Emerald Blossom, Reversion, Spiritbloom, Temporal Anomaly, Rewind and Emerald Communion when native admission and party conditions permit. When nobody is below 95%, Fire Breath, Disintegrate or Living Flame targets the tank's enemy.
