Each Thrash also extends the Rampant Thorn ticker, which deals native damage to a random primary and nearby enemies (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:5828–5857,5974–5982,11376–11381,11481–11485`).

- Wild Guardian rolls its native chance on paid Rage, cannot summon a second spirit while one is active, and applies Answered Calling with Mangle/Thrash cooldown resets when the third talent is selected (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:5090–5105,5113–5120`).

- Selected Galactic Guardian can trigger a background Moonfire from eligible native proc events and grants its Moonfire charge; a charged Moon Guardian background cast earns its native Rage (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:7871–7947,12694–12749`).
- Selected Gore can proc from the sourced direct Bear impacts, granting its native Mangle modifier with the native proc recovery (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:2303–2328,5489,11444–11445`).
- Landed Bear Thrash applies its native 192090 periodic bleed to each hit target. Selected Flashing Claws can repeat Thrash after 500 ms, including its tier effects (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:5911–5986,11893–11900`). The literal APL reads tier-set facts from native equipped sets.

- Lunar Beam selects the enemy while its native caster-centered destination remains unchanged. Its ground pulses trigger native self-heal 204069 once per second for the beam's duration (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:5366–5418`).

- Matted Fur applies its absorb on Barkskin or Survival Instincts when the talent is selected. Its amount follows the native talent coefficient, attack power, healing versatility, and recipient absorb modifiers.
- Selected Blood Frenzy earns the native rage payload on its hasted periodic clock for each target with the caster's Thrash bleed, up to the talent's native target cap. Its initial phase is randomized (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:11384–11396,13187–13191`).
- Ursoc's Guidance reduces Berserk and Incarnation cooldowns in proportion to actual paid Rage and its native divisor (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:5076–5089`).
