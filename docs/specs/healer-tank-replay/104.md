# Guardian Druid (104) replay

The SimC dungeon-dummy scenario uses a level-92 attacker, difficulty 23, content tuning 3025 and MythicPlusSeason 120. ExpectedStatMod 448 supplies its armor coefficient; older measurements below predate this scenario correction.

- Foreground Druid spells with a nonzero GCD reschedule the active main-hand swing, while form shifts and melee attacks do not (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:2513–2533,3583–3593`).
- Bear Form applies native 17057 on entry, granting 25 Rage, and discards remaining Rage on exit (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:3035–3044,3052–3062,3079–3085`).
- Bear Form applies its native passive 1178, including stamina and the swing Rage modifier, and removes it when leaving the form (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:9851–9865,14320`). Its effect 3 grants Rage from landed melee swings and specials, including absorbed hits, with the native 750 ms proc recovery (`sc_druid.cpp:12480–12500`).
- Selected Gift of the Wild increases Mark of the Wild's native versatility on the talent owner only (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:10791–10795`).
- Selected Lycara's Teachings grants its rank-scaled versatility while in Bear Form and removes it when the form ends (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:3627–3636,14344–14347`).
- Casting Guardian Berserk or Incarnation enters Bear Form before the ability executes and resets Mangle, Thrash and Growl cooldowns (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:5222–5243`). With Fluid Form, Rake and Shred enter Cat Form, while Mangle enters Bear Form (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:2082–2107,4518,4782,5428`).

- Berserk or Incarnation grants the selected Wild Guardian replacement action; using it consumes the override and triggers Dream Conduit. Maul or Raze consumes one Conduit stack and summons a spirit if none is active (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:5222–5233,5667–5676,9003–9019`).
- With an active Wild Guardian spirit, each Mangle or Thrash, including Flashing Claws repeats, executes Spirit's Wrath against a live random enemy. Its selected native Rage payload is immediate and its damage lands after 200 ms (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:5124–5143,6011–6031`).
- Equipped Guardian MID2 two-piece rolls its native chance on every Thrash, including Flashing Claws repeats, to grant Gorestained Claws for the next Mangle (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:5968–5972,11447–11449`).
- Equipped Guardian MID2 four-piece extends the current Incarnation or Berserk by the native increment on each actual Thrash cast, up to the native extension cap. Each Thrash also extends the Rampant Thorn ticker, which deals native damage to a random primary and nearby enemies (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:5828–5857,5974–5982,11376–11381,11481–11485`).

- Wild Guardian rolls its native chance on paid Rage, cannot summon a second spirit while one is active, and applies Answered Calling with Mangle/Thrash cooldown resets when the third talent is selected (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:5090–5105,5113–5120`).
- Selected Atmospheric Exposure applies the native caster-owned 6% damage-taken debuff on Lunar Beam damage, refreshed by its impacts and periodic ticks (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:2153–2181,5356–5390,14298–14307`).
- Selected Star Cascade can trigger native Starsurge at its Guardian effectiveness from a landed foreground Thrash, not a Flashing Claws repeat (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:5954,6002–6007,8601–8614`).
- Cat-form Heart of the Wild executes the native Feral Frenzy child on its 200 ms cadence, with an armor-reduced direct hit, one armor-bypassing rolling bleed stream, and the native Heart multiplier on both (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:4114–4150,9130–9170,11750–11765`).
- Selected Wild Guardian's second talent adds a rank-scaled rolling Vicious Brambles residual from each landed Mangle, Maul, Raze or Ravage impact (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:5192–5212,5428–5433,5600–5605,11959–11962`).

- Selected Galactic Guardian can trigger a background Moonfire from eligible native proc events and grants its Moonfire charge; a charged Moon Guardian background cast earns its native Rage (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:7871–7947,12694–12749`).
- Selected Gore can proc from the sourced direct Bear impacts, granting its native Mangle modifier with the native proc recovery (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:2303–2328,5489,11444–11445`).
- Landed Bear Thrash applies its native 192090 periodic bleed to each hit target. Selected Flashing Claws can repeat Thrash after 500 ms, including its tier effects (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:5911–5986,11893–11900`). The literal APL reads tier-set facts from native equipped sets.

- Lunar Beam selects the enemy while its native caster-centered destination remains unchanged. Its ground pulses deal native 414613 area damage to at most five enemies, then trigger native self-heal 204069 once per second for the beam's duration (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:5366–5418`). In the canonical trace, SimC incorrectly sends 204069 to the enemy despite the self-target initialization; native keeps the self-heal. SimC raw healing is therefore not self-healing evidence.

- Ursoc's Fury shields for the native 35% of eligible damage, scaled by healing modifiers and capped at 30% maximum health. SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:5158–5173` adds an extra 100% of damage, producing about 135% before healing modifiers instead of 35%; this is a known SimC size gap, not a native parity target.
- Matted Fur applies its absorb on Barkskin or Survival Instincts when selected. Its amount uses the rank-scaled native coefficient, total attack power including the weapon, healing versatility, and the recipient absorb multiplier once (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:2998–3001`). SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:3463–3469` instead multiplies by `1 +` its already multiplicative recipient factor: at a recipient factor of 1.0, SimC's shield is twice the native shield. This is a known SimC size gap, not a native parity target.
- Bear Thrash's 192090 bleed carries its talent-modified Rend and Tear effects: per stack, +2% damage and +2% auto-attack damage taken from the Druid and −3% damage done to the Druid. Moonfire 164812 effect 5 applies the selected Scintillating Moonlight's −12% damage done to the Druid (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:14307,14380–14390`).
- Ironfur 192081 stacks asynchronously: each cast adds one stack that expires on its own 9-second lifetime, as SimC's `ASYNCHRONOUS` buff does (`sc_druid.cpp:11012–11017`). Native data refreshes every stack on each cast.
- Selected Blood Frenzy earns the native rage payload on its hasted periodic clock for each target with the caster's Thrash bleed, up to the talent's native target cap. Its initial phase is randomized (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:11384–11396,13187–13191`).
- Ursoc's Guidance reduces Berserk and Incarnation cooldowns in proportion to actual paid Rage and its native divisor (SimC76f8118 `sc_druid.cpp:5076–5089`).

## Subsequent diagnostics

The integrated table above predates the level-92 avoidance, seasonal armor, Incarnation reset, Bear passive, Druid swing-timer, and native Ursoc shield corrections. After those changes, native seed 70448 had 88 Ironfur, 80 Mangle, and 87 foreground Thrash casts; Ursoc consumed 3,133,808 and Matted consumed 655,280. Post-absorb damage was 2,289,614 and the character survived. This single seed is not a ten-seed comparison. The 35% native Ursoc shield intentionally differs from SimC's approximately 135% shield, so zero native damage is not a valid parity requirement.

Before the swing-timer and Bear passive corrections, native seed 70448 earned 822.8 Rage from 187 swings at 4.4 each, and 324 from 108 incoming swings. The SimC trace recorded 588 Rage from 147 Bear Melee hits at 4.0 each, and 318 from 106 incoming hits. SimC applies passive 1178's -8% swing modifier and resets the main-hand timer for foreground Druid spells (`sc_druid.cpp:9851–9865,2513–2533`); both were missing natively. Its incoming-hit passive includes melee specials with a 750 ms recovery (`sc_druid.cpp:12480–12500`), whereas the native path had only 1-second autoattack handling. Native now uses the passive's sourced value and recovery for both types, including absorbed hits. Updated means and the remaining reset/cast divergences are not yet established.

## Bleed and armor fixes (2026-10-29)

The 53% bleed excess had two causes. Bear Thrash applied only its periodic effect, and Moonfire omitted effect 5, so the Rend and Tear and Scintillating Moonlight reductions were missing. The trace shows both on SimC bleed ticks: Thrash 192090#4 and Moonfire 164812#6. That fix (`deced5c80`) exposed an armor error: native refreshed every Ironfur stack on each cast, so stacks accumulated to a mean armor of about 66,000 against SimC's 16,257. Asynchronous stacks fixed that (`a28c5c2cd`).

Native is 8.4% below the reference, 1.4 standard errors of the native mean; there are no deaths with or without healing. Damage plus absorbs differs by 0.9%. Open residuals: Matted Fur absorbs 43% more with equal Barkskin casts and uptime (44.7% versus 45%), and native casts 10 fewer Ironfur.

Rend and Tear also increases the Druid's damage to Thrashed targets by up to 10%, so native DPS rose by 9.5% (one target) and 6.5% (three targets) in the train measurement. Native replay DPS is now 118,962 against SimC's 109,781 (+8.4%); before the fix the two agreed only because of offsetting errors.
