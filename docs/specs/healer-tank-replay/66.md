# Protection Paladin replay

The SimC dungeon-dummy scenario uses a level-92 attacker, difficulty 23, content tuning 3025 and MythicPlusSeason 120. ExpectedStatMod 448 supplies its armor coefficient; older measurements below predate this scenario correction.

- The pinned MID2 literal APL exercises Holy Bulwark, Shining Light, Divine Guidance and Grand Crusader's Avenger's Shield reset and Crusader's Judgment recharge. Other spell effects and proc sources still require qualification.
- Selected Divine Purpose can make a subsequent Holy Power spender free. Holy Armaments does not generate Holy Power for Protection.
- Divine Toll sends Avenger's Shield to each selected enemy and grants Holy Power per target. Each Shield replaces Strength in Adversity with the real 15-second parry buff, one stack per enemy hit.
- Divine Inspiration uses the SimC haste-scaled RPPM and alternates earned self armaments independently of hardcasts.
- Blessed Hammer's area trigger uses SimC's two-hit timing and its estimated 9-yard radius. Native create-properties 6006 are unavailable; replace this approximation when the authored orbit and actions are available. Each landed pulse grants an attack-power-scaled absorb against that enemy's next hit.
- Blessing of Dusk 1241945 applies its runtime-modified maximum reduction at every health level, matching pinned SimC `sc_paladin.cpp:4729-4732`. Native DB2 effect 0 (aura 226) supplies 10%; Protection aura 137028 effect 31 adds 10 through spell label 2344, yielding 20%. Effect 1 (aura 87) has zero base points and supplies no health curve. This replaces the earlier unsourced health-scaled approximation. The real Templar Protection combat log has no Dusk aura events and does not establish a curve.
- The equipped MID2 four-piece triggers Unrelenting Edict from Judgment, Hammer of Wrath and Blessed Hammer impacts; Edict impacts can trigger Divine Inspiration.
- Glory of the Vanguard grants Valor. Shield of the Righteous consumes it, increases its primary-target damage, and triggers Blaze of Glory on secondary targets. Its armor uses native 132403 effect 0, modified from 160% to 192% of Strength for Protection, as in `sc_paladin.cpp:4649-4659`. Selected Imbued Shield reduces magic damage taken by 12% while Shield of the Righteous is active.
- Selected Faith's Armor 406101 is triggered by Shield of the Righteous. Its native 379017 armor effect is 20%, modified to 5% for Protection by 137028 effect 14 and label 2603.
- Rite of Sanctification 433568 grants aura 433550, sourced from native weapon enchant 7143: 2% Strength and 5% armor.
- SimC applies Sanctified Plates 402964 effect 2 once during aura parsing and again in `sc_paladin.cpp:4574-4575`. Native applies the single authored effect; the extra SimC factor is a reference double count, not a native armor requirement.
- SimC's default `fake_solidarity` adds fictional allied effects that the solo native replay does not reproduce. The current solo reference sets `fake_solidarity=0` immediately after loading the player profile, before creating the tank dummy.

Native uses seeds 70448–70457 after the Dusk, Devotion, two-roll, Strength-to-parry and block-DR changes; SimC uses pinned revision `76f8118`, ten measured iterations, and its default `fake_solidarity`. These are separate random samples, not paired iterations. All ten native healed runs survived.

The damage and healing totals are over the encounter, not per-second rates. SimC absorb totals include its simulated allied Solidarity opportunities; native uses only real self effects.

The user decided on 2026-10-28 that all Paladins learn Devotion Aura 465. Its canonical SkillLineAbility row 36817 marks it NeverLearned, SpellLevel is zero, and no TraitDefinition references it as a granted, visible, or overridden spell. Its retail learning source and level remain unverified; the explicit gameplay grant uses level 1.

The native dummy armor constant is 3430 from level-92 ExpectedStat, while SimC's dungeon dummy uses 4531.03. Equal armor therefore produces more native mitigation. This difference is separate from Dusk, Devotion, and Solidarity. In the healed native sample, average swing outcomes were 53.3 parries, 31.1 blocks, and 63.6 hits per 148 swings; rates are random-sample observations, not matched event pairs. Native uses pinned SimC's Strength-to-parry conversion and diminishing-return order for Warrior, Paladin and Death Knight. Native mean sampled block chance is 30.52%, compared with 30.85% SimC buffed block. Its sources include 3% base, 7% Paladin class aura 137026, and Divine Bulwark 76671 mastery subject to block DR; Holy Shield was not selected. Shield Block is outside mastery DR for Warrior.

Native Dusk averaged 0.943% reduction at incoming-event health; SimC assumes constant 20%. Holding each native outcome and pre-shield input fixed, replacing the native rate with maximum Dusk would reduce pre-absorb damage by approximately 1,455,829 per encounter, compared with the observed 1,340,146 post-absorb damage-taken gap. This is not an exact causal decomposition: shields, health, healing and ability choices would change in a full counterfactual replay. The armor constant, Solidarity, and other simulation differences remain.

Eight of ten native no-healer runs died; two survived 300 seconds. The unhealed death-time change after dispatching taken-melee-ability procs is not attributed to a specific proc: there is no equivalent SimC no-healer reference, and the literal Word of Glory health gate and changed random sequence can change casts.
