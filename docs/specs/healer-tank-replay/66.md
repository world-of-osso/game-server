# Protection Paladin replay

- The pinned MID2 literal APL exercises Holy Bulwark, Shining Light, Divine Guidance and Grand Crusader's Avenger's Shield reset and Crusader's Judgment recharge. Other spell effects and proc sources still require qualification.
- Selected Divine Purpose can make a subsequent Holy Power spender free. Holy Armaments does not generate Holy Power for Protection.
- Divine Toll sends Avenger's Shield to each selected enemy and grants Holy Power per target. Each Shield replaces Strength in Adversity with the real 15-second parry buff, one stack per enemy hit.
- Divine Inspiration uses the SimC haste-scaled RPPM and alternates earned self armaments independently of hardcasts.
- Blessed Hammer's area trigger uses SimC's two-hit timing and its estimated 9-yard radius. Native create-properties 6006 are unavailable; replace this approximation when the authored orbit and actions are available. Each landed pulse grants an attack-power-scaled absorb against that enemy's next hit.
- Blessing of Dusk 1241945 scales its runtime-modified maximum reduction linearly with missing health, evaluated before each hit. The user chose this approximation on 2026-10-28; the actual health curve remains unsourced. Pinned SimC instead applies the maximum 20% constantly and marks that behavior as a TODO.
- The equipped MID2 four-piece triggers Unrelenting Edict from Judgment, Hammer of Wrath and Blessed Hammer impacts; Edict impacts can trigger Divine Inspiration.
- Glory of the Vanguard grants Valor. Shield of the Righteous consumes it, increases its primary-target damage, and triggers Blaze of Glory on secondary targets.
- SimC's default `fake_solidarity` adds allied Reflection of Radiance opportunities that the solo native replay does not reproduce. Its 35 Holy Bulwark–sourced Grand Crusader procs include those simulated allied effects; their separate share is not observable from that run.
