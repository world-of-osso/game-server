# Protection Paladin replay

The Patchwerk DPS reference and native DPS fixture are not a like-for-like intake scenario. The pinned reference has an attacking `Fluffy_Pillow` with melee, melee nuke, bleed and tank healing; mean damage taken is 13.24M. The native DPS fixture uses passive dummy 31146 and sampled health remains full. Their talent strings, equipped item IDs and pinned profile SHA match; the native literal APL comes from that profile. Therefore the observed DPS gap is not by itself a parity result.

The SimC dungeon-dummy scenario uses a level-92 attacker, difficulty 23, content tuning 3025 and MythicPlusSeason 120. ExpectedStatMod 448 supplies its armor coefficient; older measurements below predate this scenario correction.

- The pinned MID2 literal APL exercises Holy Bulwark, Shining Light, Divine Guidance and Grand Crusader's Avenger's Shield reset and Crusader's Judgment recharge. Other spell effects and proc sources still require qualification.
- Selected Divine Purpose can make a subsequent Holy Power spender free. Holy Armaments does not generate Holy Power for Protection.
- Learned Word of Glory Rank 2 (315921) increases self-cast Word of Glory healing by its native effect 0 percentage times the recipient's missing-health fraction. It does not apply this self-only bonus to an ally.
- Divine Toll sends Avenger's Shield to each selected enemy and grants Holy Power per target. Each Shield replaces Strength in Adversity with the real 15-second parry buff, one stack per enemy hit. Selected rank-two Ferren Marcus's Fervor increases the primary Shield hit by 20%.
- Divine Inspiration uses the SimC haste-scaled RPPM and alternates earned self armaments independently of hardcasts.
- Blessed Hammer's area trigger uses SimC's two-hit timing and its estimated 9-yard radius. Native create-properties 6006 are unavailable; replace this approximation when the authored orbit and actions are available. Each landed pulse grants an attack-power-scaled absorb against that enemy's next hit.
- Blessing of Dusk 1241945 applies its runtime-modified maximum reduction at every health level, matching pinned SimC `sc_paladin.cpp:4729-4732`. Native DB2 effect 0 (aura 226) supplies 10%; Protection aura 137028 effect 31 adds 10 through spell label 2344, yielding 20%. Effect 1 (aura 87) has zero base points and supplies no health curve. This replaces the earlier unsourced health-scaled approximation. The real Templar Protection combat log has no Dusk aura events and does not establish a curve.
- The equipped MID2 four-piece triggers Unrelenting Edict from Judgment, Hammer of Wrath and Blessed Hammer impacts; Edict impacts can trigger Divine Inspiration. Selected Greater Judgment lets Judgment and Hammer of Wrath apply owned target uses that increase affected damage by 50% for Protection.
- Glory of the Vanguard grants Valor. Shield of the Righteous consumes it, increases its primary-target damage, and triggers Blaze of Glory on secondary targets. Avenger's Shield impacts grant Bulwark of Righteous Fury, which increases the next Shield of the Righteous by its native 10% per stack. Selected Instrument of the Divine increases Shield damage by 50% for each pre-cast Holy Power above its base cost, up to two. Its armor uses native 132403 effect 0, modified from 160% to 192% of Strength for Protection, as in `sc_paladin.cpp:4649-4659`. Selected Imbued Shield reduces magic damage taken by 12% while Shield of the Righteous is active.
- Selected Faith's Armor 406101 is triggered by Shield of the Righteous. Its native 379017 armor effect is 20%, modified to 5% for Protection by 137028 effect 14 and label 2603.
- Rite of Sanctification 433568 grants aura 433550, sourced from native weapon enchant 7143: 2% Strength and 5% armor.
- SimC applies Sanctified Plates 402964 effect 2 once during aura parsing and again in `sc_paladin.cpp:4574-4575`. Native applies the single authored effect; the extra SimC factor is a reference double count, not a native armor requirement. Sacred and Lesser Weapon strikes and heals do not themselves trigger Sacred Weapon (SimC `sc_paladin.cpp:2403–2408,2444–2449,2462–2467,2490–2496`).
