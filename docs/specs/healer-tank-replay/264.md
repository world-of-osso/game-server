# Restoration Shaman healer replay

On-use item policy: [measured per-item qualification](healer-item-usage.md); unsupported timing is retained as an explicit gap.

- Triage uses native readiness for Earth Shield, Riptide, Healing Wave, Healing Surge, Chain Heal, Healing Stream Totem, Unleash Life, Spirit Link and Mana Tide when learned. Cast Riptide on cooldown and Healing Wave when the most injured ally is missing at least 80% of its uncritical native base heal; use Flame Shock, Lava Burst and Lightning Bolt against the enemy when no healing action is needed, or Chain Lightning when its native chain selector finds another enemy.
- Cloudburst Totem remains blocked: spell 157153 is absent from the canonical native database and is not selected in the sourced build.
- Healing Stream Totem uses its native summoned creature, passive pulse and one injury-prioritized target per pulse.
- Earth Shield 974 triggers heal 379 from the Shaman on its damaged holder, with the native three-second proc cooldown. WCL `cqFgBM3zTvVrRa6j` fight 35 actor 280 records 394 such heals; the shortest same-target interval is 2.988 seconds.
- Surging Totem maintains Healing Rain while its summoned totem exists. The rain includes full-health party members and excludes pets and summons. **INFERRED:** it emits two immediate rain pulses: WCL `cqFgBM3zTvVrRa6j` fight 35 actor 280 commonly records two 73921 heals per recipient at the 444995 cast timestamp (for example 27.625–27.626 s). Retire the immediate-pulse inference when an authoritative pulse model is available. Native 70448 supplies the rain cadence and the totem duration. The initial snapshot v3 lacked the summoned creature 225409 in `content_creature_template`; the refreshed snapshot adds its TDB 69497 template and model with `scripts/add_guardian_templates.py --spell 444995`.
- Selected Resurgence 16196 refunds maximum mana on direct critical Healing Wave, Riptide and Chain Heal hits. Native SpellDescriptionVariables 841 specifies 0.8%, 0.48% and 0.2%, respectively, with 101033 as the energize child. Maintain the innate Water Shield 52127; incoming melee swings can trigger 52128 at its native proc cooldown.
- Use Healing Rain or selected Surging Totem when ready, targeting the current injured ally. Use Chain Heal when at least two living allies are injured, their combined missing health is at least 80% of one uncritical native Chain Heal, and mana exceeds 35%. In the WCL reference, cast-resource samples never fall below 34.13% on a Chain Heal; the threshold is a replay policy choice, not a native restriction. WCL report `cqFgBM3zTvVrRa6j` fight 35 actor 280 casts Surging Totem 62 times in 1700.4 seconds (2.19/min); the previous party-pulse policy cast neither.
