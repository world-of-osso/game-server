Both levels are within ±5% with intervals inside it. Raw (unnormalized) deltas were not remeasured on this tree; their ±13–14 pp intervals on `83adc351f2` carry no parity information.

Two native defects closed the level-70 gap (+6.40% ± 0.99 pp on `83adc351f2`):

- **Burgeoning Seed** (item 193634, ilvl 73). DB2 384636 has only dummy auras; its tooltip consumes Brimming Life-Pod 384646 for $384658s1 versatility and $384658s3 maximum health per stack. SimC `unique_gear_dragonflight.cpp:963–1006` triggers Supernatural 384658 with the consumed stack count. Native cast the seed with no effect, so its max health — and the 10× max-health Stagger cap — stayed at 47,255 while SimC reached 48,702–53,043. Fix `8cf8fc4bc5`; four pods now add exactly 4 × 1,447 max health, the SimC trace value. Alone it moved level 70 to +5.79% ± 0.97 pp.
- **Anvil and Stave** (386937, rank 2 in the 268/70 profile). DB2 has only dummy effects; its tooltip reduces Brews by ${$s1/10} s per dodge or enemy miss. SimC `sc_monk.cpp:7245–7252` applies the rank-scaled 1 s to Purifying, Celestial, Fortifying and Black Ox Brew on each dodge (seed 70448: −1 s at 4, 6, 10, 14, 16 s). Native never reduced Brews on avoidance. After the seed fix, native Purifying Brew averaged 14.9 vs 15.8 casts, the pool capped more often, and native bleed after Stagger was +13.5% and melee +6.3% over SimC. Fix `2103d1a70b`, test `10d2b347b5`.

Native follows the DB2 tooltip and also triggers on misses; SimC triggers on dodges only. Misses average 1.1 per run against 21.4 dodges, about 1 s of extra Brew reduction per 120 s; this difference was not measured separately.

Treemouth's Festering Splinter 395175 deals 2,463 + 1,951 self-damage per level-70 run (two uses); the measured damage excludes non-periodic self-damage, but native Stagger defers part of it and the APL spends a Purifying Brew charge at 0.05 s. SimC `unique_gear_dragonflight.cpp:5002–5007` zeroes the self-damage. DB2 70448 395175 effect 1035434 and its tooltip ("immediately suffering $s1 Nature damage") author it, so native keeps it. Retail WCL could not decide whether Stagger defers it: 186 ranked Brackenhide Hollow Brewmaster runs list item 193652 (WCL encounters 12520/62520), but their reports are archived and the client API refuses their events (`xy3bwHmFG1pDgACr` fight 5, `Lq1nTAzgGBFtdM9N` fight 93, `rWyH9tZPmfKdaM6Y` fight 4). The control row zeroes 395175 damage before Stagger (`control-zero-treemouth-self.patch`, not committed); it changes level 70 by 0.05 pp.

**Retail Calming Presence aura visibility remains unverified.** WCL Midnight Season 2 ranking talent data selects entry 124944 for 15 sampled Brewmasters, but none of their combatantinfo aura lists contains 388664. For `dyPvYNj83HgDrMcF` fight 12 actor 192, `6GP7NtzJ8b19Mram` fight 11 actor 1 and `c6YhHaA9RTM1FXKq` fight 5 actor 4, queried Buffs events also contain no 388664. Absence from those logs does not prove the passive is inactive; DB2 passives often do not appear in combatantinfo. The adjusted SimC is a DB2-based control.

The expected run replaces each unmitigated dummy swing's damage with its raw amount times the capped landed probability from SimC's `build_table`, before armor, Stagger and shields. It preserves the sampled outcome for avoidance-triggered effects. Each implementation then executes its ordinary Stagger ticks and Purifying Brew, including the selected percentage and minimum clear; the test verifies that a 50%-weighted dodged swing enters the pool and a 60% Purify clears the same fraction of weighted and full pools. Sampled outcomes still affect later proc state, so this is a pre-Stagger expected-damage control, not an exhaustive integration over all possible future proc states. Each trace has 29 matched swing probabilities; all native receipts have zero unknown/non-unit consumed-absorb points. The SimC target damage and self-Stagger components sum to reported damage in every run.

The level-70 delta was outside ±5%; the section above attributes and fixes it.

The old unknown-scale absorb points were consumed during self-Stagger ticks, not unscaled enemy hits. In level-70 seed 70448 the 69,059 unknown points equal the recorded self-Stagger absorption; in level 90 the sole consumed shield is Celestial Infusion 1241059. The capture now derives the self-hit scale from the actual attacker and target, while retaining the recorded enemy-hit scale for other attacks. A real self-Stagger tick consuming Celestial Infusion passes the behavioral test.

Receipts, `simc-calming-control.patch`, `simc-expected-control.patch`, the WCL combatantinfo and Buffs responses, and `summarize-expected.py` are under `game-engine/data/diagnostics/tank-parity-brm-prot-2026-11-01/`.
