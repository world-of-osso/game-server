# Discipline Priest (256) healer replay

The replay uses a native healing priority, not a SimC healer APL: SimC `76f8118` supplies no Priest healer APL. Shield maintains the tank's Atonement; the policy fills available globals with Penance, Mind Blast and Smite against the enemy and saves defensive cooldowns for low health.

Atonement applies from Shield, Radiance and Renew and transfers damage into healing using native 81749 effect 0. Shield uses the native spell 17 absorb and spell-power coefficient. The gameplay test in `crates/server-spell-tests/src/spell_cast/discipline_atonement_tests.rs` exercises an absorbed hit and a real Smite heal.
