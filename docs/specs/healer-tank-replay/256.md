# Discipline Priest (256) healer replay

The replay uses a native healing priority, not the experimental SimC healer APL. SimC `76f8118` contains a Discipline APL in `engine/class_modules/apl/apl_priest.cpp:216–256`, but its Priest healer role is explicitly unsupported in `sc_priest.cpp:3805–3816`. Shield maintains the tank's Atonement; the policy fills available globals with Penance, Mind Blast and Smite against the enemy and saves defensive cooldowns for low health.

Atonement applies from Shield, Radiance, Flash Heal and Renew and transfers damage into healing using native 81749 effect 0. Shield uses the native spell 17 absorb and spell-power coefficient. The gameplay tests in `crates/server-spell-tests/src/spell_cast/discipline_atonement_tests.rs` exercise Shield, a critical Smite against a partly absorbed target, and a non-triggering Fireball. Damage transfers use the native pre-absorb source amount, retain its critical result and apply native Grace mastery to marked targets. Shadow Abyssal Reverie, Holy Prophet's Insight and active Voidheart use their native effect values; the registered source actions follow the pinned SimC trigger list. Pet transfers and target-count rules remain open.

## Reference and open gaps (2026-10-29)

The top-character profile with the default experimental APL fails in SimC: `evangelism_radiance` has spell data 0. SimC registers Radiance, Pain Suppression, Penance, Evangelism and Ultimate Penitence as Discipline actions, and shared Shield, Void Shield, Flash Heal, Smite, Mind Blast, Halo, Holy Nova and Shadow Word: Death. Its class module implements Atonement and Shield absorbs; registration is not proof that an action works with this profile. Renew has a class implementation but no action registration. Do not infer missing spell behavior from the APL alone.
