# Discipline Priest (256) healer replay

The replay uses a native healing priority, not the experimental SimC healer APL. SimC `76f8118` contains a Discipline APL in `engine/class_modules/apl/apl_priest.cpp:216–256`, but its Priest healer role is explicitly unsupported in `sc_priest.cpp:3805–3816`. The Shield list follows the pinned APL's active-Atonement, Penance cooldown and Master the Darkness gates, preferring an Atoned ally with the shortest remaining mark and a self penalty. Borrowed Time and mana conditions govern ordinary Shield; Void Shield uses the Master the Darkness proc. The policy also uses Flash Heal or its selected Shadow Mend override on the ally with the shortest Atonement remaining (with the APL's self penalty), under the pinned APL's GCD and mana conditions. It fills available globals with Penance, Mind Blast and Smite against the enemy and saves defensive cooldowns for low health.

Atonement applies from Shield, Radiance, Flash Heal and Renew and transfers damage into healing using native 81749 effect 0. Shield uses the native spell 17 absorb and spell-power coefficient. The gameplay tests in `crates/server-spell-tests/src/spell_cast/discipline_atonement_tests.rs` exercise Shield, a critical Smite against a partly absorbed target, and a non-triggering Fireball. Damage transfers use the native pre-absorb source amount, retain its critical result and apply native Grace mastery to marked targets. Shadow Abyssal Reverie, Holy Prophet's Insight and active Voidheart use their native effect values; the registered source actions follow the pinned SimC trigger list. A real-Smite test covers SimC's separate injured and full-health recipient soft caps. Real-summon tests cover Shadowfiend, Mindbender and Voidwraith damage and Atonement.

Selected Holy Ray 372969 increases later hostile Penance 47666 bolts through 1235193. Selected Twinsight 440742 emits a healing bolt for each of the first four hostile Penance bolts; both bolt types advance Holy Ray, as in SimC's zero-indexed channel. Prompt Prognosis 1246799 adds 125% to the third bolt. Power of the Dark Side 198068 procs from real Pain ticks and its 198069 buff is consumed and snapshotted across the channel. The healing missile currently uses native impact-time modifiers rather than SimC's launch snapshot. Friendly Penance and extra bolts beyond the first four remain outside this coverage.

Evangelism 472433 triggers Radiance at 150% effectiveness using native 70448 values; the pinned SimC source describes that child, but the experimental action fails initialization. The replay places Evangelism and Radiance at the pinned APL's priorities and uses Atonement-remaining targeting. The selected profile learns Evangelism but not Radiance, so only the triggered Radiance is available for that profile.

Divine Aegis 47753 accumulates 30% of eligible effective critical direct heals, capped at 30% of recipient maximum health. It does not trigger from Atonement; actual absorbed damage is attributed to the healer and shield spell. Shadow Mend 1252217 overrides Flash Heal with 186263; Shadow Mend applies Atonement. WCL report `VxFtGRafyAbKmnd9` fight 10 actor 3 shows same-target Atonement apply/refresh within 150 ms for 139 of 139 Shadow Mend casts; SimC marks Shadow Mend NYI.

The selected Soulcoiler Ritual Vessel 270162 uses its native two-second channel and item-level-scaled equipped driver; each half-second tick, including the initial tick, gives a random unshielded ally a 1307578 barrier. The barrier absorbs 50% of each hit until its capacity is consumed or its native 20-second duration expires. SimC applies the source role multiplier to the item amount.

Shield Discipline 197045 returns mana through 47755 when a Shield or Void Shield ends before its duration expires. A landed Shadowfiend melee returns mana through 343727; Mindbender uses 123051. The selected profile has Shield Discipline and Shadowfiend. Real shield-depletion and real summoned-fiend tests cover these returns.

## Reference and open gaps (2026-10-29)

The top-character profile with the default experimental APL fails in SimC: `evangelism_radiance` has spell data 0. SimC registers Radiance, Pain Suppression, Penance, Evangelism and Ultimate Penitence as Discipline actions, and shared Shield, Void Shield, Flash Heal, Smite, Mind Blast, Halo, Holy Nova and Shadow Word: Death. Its class module implements Atonement and Shield absorbs; registration is not proof that an action works with this profile. Renew has a class implementation but no action registration. Do not infer missing spell behavior from the APL alone.
