# Maps, map transfers and dungeon instances (server)

Dungeons phases 1 and 4: several maps in one world, far teleports between them, dungeon copies per group, dungeon difficulties and instance locks. Code: `crates/server/src/maps/` (`difficulty.rs`, `locks.rs` for phase 4). Protocol: shared-protocol `protocol/transfer_messages.rs` (`NewWorld`, `WorldPortAck`, `TransferAborted`), `protocol/instance_messages.rs` (difficulty, locks, resets). Client: game-engine `docs/specs/instances.md`. Behavior reference: TrinityCore master a352b1fa (functions cited per rule).

## What it must do

### Maps in one world
- [x] Every world entity carries `InMap { map_id, instance_id }` (`WorldObject::GetMapId` / `GetInstanceId`); continents are instance 0.
- [x] Eastern Kingdoms (0), Kalimdor (1) and reconstructed [Zephras (2991)](zephras-content.md) are hosted (`maps::HOSTED_CONTINENTS`). Other world maps are refused (`MapNotAllowed`). Creature copies use imported map/difficulty data without a client-appearance allowlist ([instance population](retail-world-content.md#instance-population-2026-10-11)). Zephras uses explicitly labelled, user-approved class-1 health estimates for missing NPC stats; unavailable individual client appearances remain withheld. This is reconstructed gameplay, not authored NPC-stat parity.
- [x] Visibility, creature aggro (`IsInMap` in `_IsTargetAcceptable`), spell area and explicit targets (`ObjectAccessor::GetUnit` searches the caster's map), grid activation, say/yell/emote range and group XP, quest and loot credit (`IsAtGroupRewardDistance`) only relate entities of one map copy. A creature whose victim leaves its map loses it and evades.
- [x] Player ground is per map: the map's `Map.db2` WDT either names one global WMO (MPHD 0x1; the Stockade, WMO 108631 placed from the world origin) or ADT tiles.
- [x] A living player below `Map::GetMinHeight` dies of `DAMAGE_FALL_TO_VOID` (MovementHandler.cpp:594-612) and is `OutOfBounds` until above it again. The minimum is -500 on a WMO-only map (the Stockade) and on tiles without MFBO; elsewhere it is the root ADT's MFBO minimum plane (`GridMap::getMinHeight`).
- [x] The character's map is saved (`character_maps`) with its position; a player in a transfer is saved at its destination (`m_teleport_dest`). Characters without a row are on map 0.

### Teleports (`Player::TeleportTo`, Player.cpp:1225-1466)
- [x] Same map: the player moves at once (`MovementControl` epoch).
- [x] Other map: `Map::PlayerCannotEnter` (Map.cpp:1807-1852) is checked first; a refusal sends `TransferAborted` and the player stays. Otherwise its selection and combat stop, its cast is cancelled, its taxi flight ends, it leaves its map (no `InMap`, `MapTransfer`) and gets `NewWorld { map_id, map_directory, position, facing }`.
- [x] `WorldPortAck` (`HandleMoveWorldportAck`, MovementHandler.cpp:189-384) adds the player to the copy `MapManager::CreateMap` gives it, after that copy's `CannotEnter`; a refused arrival sends `TransferAborted` and returns the player where it left from (TrinityCore uses the homebind, which the server has no data for). `TELE_REVIVE_AT_TELEPORT` resurrects on arrival, and so does entering the dungeon that holds the player's corpse.
- [x] The arrival raises the player's replicated `WorldArrival` count in the frame the interest update makes the destination's objects visible, so it reaches the client in their replication tick and never before them; the client ends its loading screen on it.
- [x] A player in a transfer does not move, fall or fight.

### Dungeon copies (`MapManager::CreateMap` MapManager.cpp:154-235, `FindInstanceIdForPlayer` :278-319)

- Dungeon login after a copy resets, dungeon release when no graveyard or exit exists, and the follower catalog use the same entrance: the incoming authored portal target first, then the map's imported entrance safe location. A missing entrance fails explicitly.
- [x] Five-player dungeons (`InstanceType` 1): a copy is created at the entrant's dungeon difficulty (the group's in a group), downscaled to one the map's `MapDifficulty.db2` offers (`GetDownscaledMapDifficultyData`: the difficulty, else its `Difficulty.db2` `FallbackDifficultyID` chain Mythic → Heroic → Normal, else the map's default). The Stockade (Normal only) opens a Normal copy at Heroic and Mythic; a map offering none answers `TRANSFER_ABORT_DIFFICULTY` ("Heroic difficulty mode is not available for %s."). Scenarios and battlegrounds are refused (`MapNotAllowed`); [raid entry](raid-entry.md) extends the same manager for raids.
- [x] Without a reset schedule (`ResetInterval` 0: Normal) a group enters its most recent copy of the map (`Group::GetRecentInstanceId`), a player without a group its own (`Player::GetRecentInstanceId`); without one a copy is created and remembered. Deviation: a remembered copy at another difficulty is not re-entered (TrinityCore would enter it when a difficulty change could not reset it).
- [x] With a reset schedule the instance owner's lock names the copy (see Instance locks); the owner is the player, or the group's recent instance owner, else its leader (`Group::GetRecentInstanceOwner`).
- [x] Level conditions (`Player::Satisfy`, Player.cpp:20055-20131, TrinityCore master 7fd1b810): before a far teleport into a dungeon, the player's level must meet every `MapDifficultyXCondition.db2` of the downscaled `MapDifficulty` (in `OrderIndex` order) whose `PlayerCondition.db2` names a ContentTuning: at least its `content_tuning_level` minimum, at most its maximum unless `WithinOrAboveRecord` (0x20); `Disabled` (0x100) conditions are met (`IsPlayerMeetingCondition`, ConditionMgr.cpp:2917-2953). The first failed condition answers `TransferAbortReason::Condition` with the condition's failure text (the `MapDifficultyXCondition`'s, else the `PlayerCondition`'s), `$pcMinLevel` resolved to the ContentTuning minimum; the player stays where it was. Stormwind Stockade (MapDifficulty 8 → PlayerCondition 74354, ContentTuning 215: levels 10-30, flags 0x60) refuses levels below 10 with "You must be at least level 10 to enter Stormwind Stockade." and admits level 10. A dungeon without a ContentTuning condition (The Culling of Stratholme, 595) admits level 1. Not evaluated: `PlayerCondition.MinLevel` (TrinityCore ignores it; Stockade's is the legacy 15), ModifierTree/aura conditions (boosted characters, class trials, Timerunners, which the server has none of), `InvertContentTuning` / `IncludeLevelDelta` (no party dungeon condition uses them), `access_requirement` (TDB1210's rows for the hosted dungeons are all zero), effective level (no level sync: the level is used).
- [x] `InstanceMap::CannotEnter` (Map.cpp:2908-2946): a full copy (`MapDifficulty.MaxPlayers`, 5 for the Stockade) or one owned by another group answers `TRANSFER_ABORT_MAX_PLAYERS`.
- [x] A copy starts its unload timer when created and when its last player leaves (`InstanceMap::RemovePlayerFromMap`); after `Instance.UnloadDelay` (30 min) it is removed with its entities, and the next entry recreates it fresh under the same id.
- [x] Each Stockade copy spawns its own Retail creatures (143, TDB1210 Normal; [retail world content](retail-world-content.md)) and game objects (34: fires and a bonfire, non-usable decoration) when it is created, with the generic creature AI (boss scripts: phase 3); ordinary creatures respawn inside their copy; bosses whose encounter is `DONE` remain dead while that copy is loaded, and saved completion governs later reloads. Other imported dungeons also spawn their creatures; missing individual client receipts/appearance coverage remain rendering gaps, not server population gates.
- [x] A copy spawns the map's creatures and game objects whose `spawnDifficulties` include its difficulty, each creature with its entry's template at that difficulty (`content_creature_template_difficulty`: ContentTuning, levels, scaling expansion, level delta, health/mana/armor/damage modifiers, loot and money), else its base template. Grim Batol (670) at Normal and Heroic: 529 creatures each; Trogg Dweller HealthModifier 0.4 (Normal) vs 1.125 (Heroic). Mythic Grim Batol opens empty: TDB1210 has no Mythic Grim Batol spawns and TrinityCore does not fall back for spawns.

### Dungeon difficulty (`HandleSetDungeonDifficultyOpcode`, MiscHandler.cpp:936-996)
- [x] `SetDungeonDifficulty` sets the player's dungeon difficulty (`Player::SetDungeonDifficultyID`) to a `Difficulty.db2` row of `InstanceType` party with `DIFFICULTY_FLAG_CAN_SELECT` (Normal 1, Heroic 2, Mythic 23; Mythic Keystone 8 is refused), not while the player is in a dungeon; saved per character (`character_dungeon_difficulty`), sent at login and on change (`DungeonDifficultySet`). New characters are Normal.
- [x] In a group only the leader changes it (`Group::SetDungeonDifficultyID`); every member takes the group's difficulty (as `Group::AddMember` does for a joiner) and gets `DungeonDifficultySet`. A group starts at its leader's difficulty (`Group::Create`). Members inside an instance stay in their copy (TrinityCore and Retail allow the change while members are inside; `LAIRS_DIFFICULTY_CHANGED_WARNING`).
- [x] A change resets the player's (group's) copies without players and without a lock in use and forgets them (`ResetInstances(OnChangeDifficulty)`; `InstanceReset` "%s has been reset.").
- [x] A player added to a map gets `WorldServerInfo { map_id, difficulty_id }` (0 on a continent), the client's `GetInstanceInfo` difficulty.

### Instance locks (`InstanceLockMgr`, InstanceLockMgr.cpp; `InstanceMap::UpdateInstanceLock`, Map.cpp:3163-3211)
- [x] A difficulty with a reset schedule locks: Grim Batol Heroic daily (`MapDifficulty` 1: `ResetInterval` 1, `LockID` 0, flags 0), Mythic weekly (`MapDifficulty` 3345: `ResetInterval` 2, `LockID` 251, flags 0x42 `UseLootBasedLockInsteadOfInstanceLock`). Normal never locks (`ResetInterval` 0). Locks are keyed by character and (`MapID`, `LockID`).
- [x] A copy created for an owner without an active lock gives it a temporary lock (`CreateInstanceLockForNewInstance`). A boss becoming `DONE` saves every player in the copy (`UpdateInstanceLockForPlayer`): the temporary lock becomes permanent (else a new one, replacing an expired one), records the boss states and the killed encounter's `DungeonEncounter.Bit` (Umbriss 1051 → bit 3), expires at the next reset; first-time saves get `InstanceSaveCreated` ("You are now saved to this instance"). Instance-id-bound locks (no encounter-lock flag) also update the copy's shared state (`UpdateSharedInstanceLock`).
- [x] Reset times are Retail's (US): daily at 15:00 UTC, weekly on Tuesday 15:00 UTC (`GetNextResetTime` with those in place of `ResetSchedule.Hour`/`WeekDay`). An expired, unextended lock no longer binds (`FindActiveInstanceLock`); the next boss kill starts a fresh lock.
- [x] Entering with an active instance-id-bound lock puts the player in its copy; a copy unloaded meanwhile is recreated under the same id with the saved boss states (`CreateInstanceData` → `Load`), and bosses saved `DONE` do not spawn (TrinityCore keeps them dead through their saved respawn time). A player saved to another copy of that difficulty cannot enter this one (`CanJoinInstanceLock`: `TRANSFER_ABORT_LOCKED_TO_DIFFERENT_INSTANCE`, "You are already locked to %s."). Encounter locks bind to no copy; a grouped encounter-lock copy starts fresh.
- [x] Encounter doors follow their bosses (`LoadDoorData`/`UpdateDoorState`): a ported script's door entry opens (`GO_STATE_ACTIVE`, no collision) only while every encounter bound to it allows it, on each boss state change and when the door spawns. Doors are keyed by `DungeonEncounter` ID. Ported: Ruby Life Pools Fire Wall 377194 opens when Kokia (2606) is `DONE`.
- Door state changes replicate the authored Open (148) or Close (146) one-shot. An already-open door initializes in Opened (149), without replaying Open; an already-closed door initializes in Stand (0). A client joining an existing door after it opened replays Open once before Opened.
- [x] A copy whose lock reset passes is reset (`InstanceMap::Update` → `Reset(Expire)`): players inside get `RaidInstanceMessage { Expired }` ("Your instance lock for %s has expired."), an empty copy unloads at once (`MIN_UNLOAD_DELAY`), and the last player leaving an expired copy unloads it at once.
- [x] `RequestRaidInfo` answers `InstanceInfo` (`SendRaidInfo`): every permanent lock with map, difficulty, instance id, seconds left (`GetEffectiveExpiryTime`), completed mask, locked and extended.
- [x] `SetSavedInstanceExtend` extends (or unextends) a lock of an extendable difficulty (`DisableLockExtension` unset) by one reset period (`UpdateInstanceLockExtensionForPlayer`); an extended lock stays active past its reset.
- [x] `ResetInstances` (a group's leader, or a player alone) resets its copies without players and without a used lock (`InstanceMap::Reset`: `InstanceReset`, or `InstanceResetFailed` while players are inside).
- [x] Locks and shared states persist in game.redb (`instance_locks`) and load at startup, their instance ids not reused (`InstanceLockMgr::Load`, `RegisterInstanceId`).
- [x] `game-server-admin clock-advance <seconds>` shifts the lock clock (`GameTime::GetSystemTime`); `instance-locks <name>` lists a character's locks (`.instance listbinds`); `instance-unbind <name> <map|all>` expires its unused locks at the last reset (`ResetInstanceLocksForPlayer`, `.instance unbind`).

### Area triggers (`HandleAreaTriggerOpcode`, MiscHandler.cpp:479-640)
- [x] `AreaTrigger.db2` sphere and box triggers with an `areatrigger_teleport` row (`world_safe_locs` target) teleport a player who walks into them (`Player::IsInAreaTrigger`: `IsInDist`, `Position::IsWithinBox`). A ghost uses only triggers whose `AreaTriggerActionSet` allows ghosts; a taxi passenger none. The first position on a map records the trigger stood in without firing.
- [x] Stockade: trigger 101 in Stormwind → safe loc 3599 (56.68, 0.62, -19.27) on map 34; trigger 503 inside → safe loc 3618 (-8766.11, 845.50, 88.00) on map 0.

### Dungeon deaths (`Player::RepopAtGraveyard`, Player.cpp:4720-4764)
- [x] Releasing in a dungeon turns the corpse to bones and teleports the player, revived at half health (`TELE_REVIVE_AT_TELEPORT`), to the graveyard `ObjectMgr::GetClosestGraveyardInZone` (ObjectMgr.cpp:6868-6970) picks for the dungeon's zone: same or parent map by 3D distance, else on `CorpseMapID` closest to `Corpse`, else any. A WMO-only dungeon's zone is its `Map.db2` AreaTableID (`TerrainInfo::GetAreaId`). Stockade (zone 717) → graveyard 1854 (-8786.88, 829.08, 97.65) in Stormwind.
- [x] If a dungeon zone has no imported graveyard, the approved rule revives the player at the exterior safe location reached by its linked exit portal, on the same outside map as its entrance. Prefer `AreaTrigger.db2`/`areatrigger_teleport` links; world-static teleport actions also supply links. For map 2521, entrance spawn 19 on map 2444 targets safe-loc 100030 inside; exit spawn 20 targets safe-loc 100031 on map 2444 at (1341.3, -132.202, 137.45). This is an approved Retail-oriented rule, not TC's default-faction-graveyard behavior. If the reciprocal links are absent, release fails explicitly.
- [x] Continent deaths keep the spirit-healer graveyards on the player's own map (Kalimdor deaths release to Kalimdor healers).

### Login (`Player::LoadFromDB`, Player.cpp:18480-18545)
- [x] A character enters its saved map's copy (`CreateMap`); a dungeon copy that refuses it, or cannot be created, sends it to the go-back trigger target (`GetGoBackTrigger`); a newly created dungeon copy puts it at the entrance (`GetMapEntranceTrigger`). Uldaman map 2451 has no incoming trigger in the imported data; use its authored TDB WorldSafeLoc 100032 entrance. If an entrance trigger or assigned safe location is missing, refuse login rather than reuse a stale position; retries must not reuse an empty copy created by the failed login. `LoadTerrain` names the map's directory.

### Meeting stones across maps
- [x] An accepted summon teleports the target to the summoner's map and position (`TeleportTo(m_summon_location)`), with a map transfer when they differ.

### Admin
- [x] `game-server-admin teleport <name> <map> <x> <y> <z>` (`.go xyz`): online characters teleport (transfer across maps), offline ones are saved there.
- [x] `game-server-admin kill <name>` (`.die`).

## Gaps
- Raid admission/difficulty now follow [raid entry](raid-entry.md); `access_requirement` checks and Dungeon Finder entry remain separate gaps.
- No pending lock when entering a copy already in progress (`PendingRaidLock`, 60 s `INSTANCE_LOCK_TIMER`, `InstanceLockResponse`): a player entering a copy with killed bosses is saved only at the next kill.
- Grouped Mythic (encounter locks) does not mark killed encounters ineligible (`TRANSFER_ABORT_ALREADY_COMPLETED_ENCOUNTER` applies to flex raids only in TrinityCore) and has no loot lockout.
- No lock warnings (`RAID_INSTANCE_WARNING_*`) and no `RAID_INSTANCE_WELCOME`; no hourly instance limit (`TRANSFER_ABORT_TOO_MANY_INSTANCES`).
- No instance boot when a player leaves the owning group inside (`Player::CheckInstanceValidity` timer).
- `graveyard_zone` conditions (team, phase) and AreaTable parent-zone fallback are not imported; polygon and cylinder area triggers are not loaded (no teleport trigger uses them).
- TrinityCore's homebind fallbacks: no homebind data.
- Players carry no `Zone`; the zone of a continent position is not computed.
