Population: 955 player-key observations in 191 unique Midnight Season 2 Ruby Life Pools report/fights; five combatant-info records per key. Selection: the 200 existing per-spec/key-band WCL anchors, deduplicated by report/fight. Samples are not independent players or a random key population.

Each cell shows the modal **observed** spell/enchant ID, its count/total player-keys, and the number with any observation in that category. Unobserved is not assumed to mean unused. Equal top counts are labeled ties. Potion choices count player-keys with one or more casts of each searched combat potion; a player-key can contribute to multiple choices. The potion columns show any searched combat potion use and median first cast seconds from key start. Exact quality is not established by these spell IDs. All choices, counts, percentages, per-choice report/fight/actor citations and non-observations are in [choice distributions](choice-distributions.md). ## Limits

- Combatant-info is a key-start snapshot. A missing aura or temporary enchant is recorded as unobserved, not proven absent throughout the run. Mid-key application, expiry and class weapon abilities are not inferred as consumables.
- Potion scan is limited to IDs 1236994, 1236998, 1236616, 1295132, 1239479, 1238443, 1307435 and 1308593. Mana and health potion casts are retained separately in the JSON. Other spells/items and potion quality are not proven absent.
- A potion first-cast timestamp and count do not establish when or why a follower should use it. Pull identities, combat state, cooldowns and boss timing require separate analysis before proposing an automatic usage rule.
- Identical Well Fed names can represent different spell IDs and effects. Labels in the overview are IDs, not claims about which food item was consumed. The source snapshot does not distinguish corresponding item identities or quality.
- Candidate reports were selected for existing gear qualification, not as a random population. Some specs have low sample sizes. Augmentation Evoker is present in the data even if the current follower driver registry excludes it.
